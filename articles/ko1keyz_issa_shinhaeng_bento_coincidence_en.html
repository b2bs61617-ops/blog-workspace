ISSA (Yanagiya Issa) of KO1KEYZ shared a photo of his lunch box on his own SNS, and it quickly became a small talking point among fans.
Curious, we looked into it further and found that SHINHAENG (Oh Sin-hyeon) posted around the same time that he, too, had properly eaten a bento, leading fans on X to react with surprise at the coincidence.
This article looks at what was inside ISSA's bento, the coincidental overlap with SHINHAENG, and how fans reacted on X.

What was the post? ISSA's bento photo

Posted by:ISSA (Yanagiya Issa)

Content:A single photo of a lunch box, sent around lunchtime

Caption:"Rice! This chicken nanban looks tasty"

The story started with a single photo attached to a message ISSA sent to fans.
Alongside the short caption "Rice! This chicken nanban looks tasty," the photo showed a bento in a black plastic container, giving the impression of him happily introducing the lunch he was about to eat.
An X user who saw the post shared a screenshot of it on X, so many fans likely came across it that way.

What was inside the bento?

Zooming in on the photo, the bento turned out to be quite a hearty spread.
The rice was white rice topped with black sesame seeds, taking up about half the container, with four pieces of fried chicken coated in a sweet-tangy glaze (in a chicken nanban style) packed in below.
The side dishes included what looked like taro or burdock root simmered with edamame in a sweet-savory sauce, a green pickled vegetable, and an orange slice resembling pumpkin, along with a separate cup of what appeared to be tartar sauce.
With its rich colors and variety, it gave the impression of a hearty, satisfying meal fitting for ISSA, who is still in his growth years.

Neither the product name nor where the bento was purchased has been explained by ISSA himself, so this remains unconfirmed at this time.
As introduced below, one reaction on X mentioned buying "this" when riding the shinkansen, but that is simply one fan's personal impression and does not confirm where the bento actually came from.

Did SHINHAENG eat the same bento? A surprising coincidence spreads on X

Things took an unexpected turn when an X user replied to ISSA's bento photo with something along the lines of "isn't this Issa's bento?"
Shortly after, an account following SHINHAENG's posts wrote that SHINHAENG had shared something similar, sparking chatter on X about whether the two members had eaten similar bentos around the same time.
Looking at SHINHAENG's own posts, he did in fact post on the same day that he had "properly eaten a bento" that day. No photo was attached, but given how close the timing was, fans enjoyed the coincidence of "a similar bento with no coordination involved."

One thing worth noting is that SHINHAENG's own post did not include a photo of the bento itself.

Coincidence or not, fans found it a heartwarming little moment that the two seemed to be having lunch around the same time.

Who are ISSA and SHINHAENG?

ISSA (Yanagiya Issa)

Born March 10, 2005, from Gunma Prefecture, 177cm tall. His member color is light blue.
He played baseball exclusively from elementary through high school, even being selected for Japan's national team at the U14 Pony Asia Championship in his third year of junior high, giving him a sporty, athletic image within the group.

SHINHAENG (Oh Sin-hyeon)

Born May 3, 2004, from Mokpo, South Korea, 177cm tall. His member color is brown.
He has an unusual background, having run as an independent candidate for a local council seat in Muan County, South Korea, at age 18 (he lost), a run that was even reported by overseas media as the youngest candidate in the country.

Both members stand at 177cm, but their paths could not be more different: ISSA devoted himself to baseball back in Japan, while SHINHAENG once challenged himself in South Korean local politics.
That two members with such different backgrounds happened to be eating similar lunches at the same moment is the kind of small overlap born out of group life that fans just couldn't let slide.
