What was the post? ISSA's bento photo

Posted by:ISSA (Yanagiya Issa)

Content:A single photo of a lunch box, sent around lunchtime

Caption:"Rice! This chicken nanban looks tasty"

The story started with a single photo attached to a message ISSA sent to fans.
Alongside the short caption "Rice! This chicken nanban looks tasty," the photo showed a bento in a black plastic container, giving the impression of him happily introducing the lunch he was about to eat.
An X user who saw the post shared a screenshot of it on X, so many fans likely came across it that way.

The bento's identity: Tsukadanojo's "Tsukadama Tartar Young Chicken Nanban Bento"

Zooming in on the photo, the bento turned out to be quite a hearty spread.
The rice was white rice topped with black sesame seeds, taking up about half the container, with sweet-tangy glazed young chicken nanban packed in below.
The side dishes included nikujaga (potato, carrot, and beef simmered in a sweet-savory sauce) with edamame, a green pickled vegetable resembling takana mentaiko, a hand-rolled thick omelet, and a separate cup of egg-rich tartar sauce.
Comparing this layout, the container shape, and the way the tartar sauce was presented against official product photos from Tsukada Farm's (Tsukadanojo) bento shop, all five elements — the black sesame rice, nikujaga, takana mentaiko, thick omelet, chicken nanban, and tartar sauce — matched exactly.
This bento appears, without much doubt, to be Tsukadanojo's signature item, the "Tsukadama Tartar Young Chicken Nanban Bento."

The tartar sauce in particular is a signature touch made generously with Tsukadanojo's own premium "Tsukadama" eggs, which is where the product's name comes from.
Having won gold at the Japan Location Bento Awards three years running (2024, 2025, and 2026), it's reportedly a bento well known among entertainment industry staff as catering for film and TV shoots.
That raises the possibility that ISSA, too, was eating this bento as catering or a treat brought to a work location.

Since it's sold as a signature item at the GranSta store inside Tokyo Station, the X reaction mentioning buying "this" when riding the shinkansen makes a lot of sense for this particular bento.
Being easy to pick up right near the shinkansen ticket gates may well be part of its appeal.

Did SHINHAENG eat the same bento? A surprising coincidence spreads on X

Things took an unexpected turn when an X user replied to ISSA's bento photo with something along the lines of "isn't this Issa's bento?"
Shortly after, an account following SHINHAENG's posts wrote that SHINHAENG had shared something similar, sparking chatter on X about whether the two members had eaten similar bentos around the same time.
Looking at SHINHAENG's own posts, he did in fact post on the same day that he had "properly eaten a bento" that day. No photo was attached, but given how close the timing was, fans enjoyed the coincidence of "a similar bento with no coordination involved."
