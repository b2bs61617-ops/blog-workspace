<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What kind of brand is Miu Miu?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Miu Miu is the sister label of the Italian luxury house PRADA.<br>
It was founded in 1993 by designer Miuccia Prada, and the name comes from her own childhood nickname, "Miu."<br>
It has a more playful, slightly girlish and retro mood than Prada, and its profile has risen sharply in recent years, including being named the fashion industry's most influential brand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Although it started as a womenswear-focused brand, the past few years have seen more pieces that read as menswear or genderless.<br>
A basic white shirt like this logo-embroidered poplin style, wearable regardless of gender, is a prime example.<br>
It's often worn by K-pop idols and actors, and it's popular as "a subtle logo that the people who know, know."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
