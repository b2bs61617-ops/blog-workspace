ViVi, Gekkan The Television, Nikkei Entertainment!, Saikyo Jump, S Cawaii! ME, and Bessatsu Kadokawa Scene have all confirmed cover or major feature placements, with releases running from September 24 to October 5, 2026 across all six titles.

ViVi Nov/Dec Special Edition: Cover & 10-Page Feature

Release date:September 24, 2026 (Thu), planned

Price:¥920 (tax included)

Content:Cover & 10-page feature, special bound-in bonus

The cover in question is a "Special Edition," which uses a different cover and bonus set from the standard and extra editions.
The issue introduces KO1KEYZ with the tagline "The most-watched global boy group, summoned by ViVi faster than anywhere else in the world," giving them a cover plus 10-page feature.
The bound-in bonus includes two items: a record-design coaster ("fly off into behind-the-scenes shots") and a flower photo album ("get your KO1 fix"), making it a must-have for fans alongside the feature itself.

The same issue also marks the 9-member group &TEAM's first-ever appearance in ViVi with a 10-page feature, a 4-page feature on Megu Hata, and a 51-page special on Korean culture, making it a star-studded issue overall.

Gekkan The Television "KO1KEYZ Limited Ver."

Release date:September 24, 2026 (Thu)

Pre-orders open:August 10, 2026 (Mon)

Content:Limited-edition-only 16-page mini book & pin-ups

Gekkan The Television includes a 4-page "Debut Gravure in Love" feature in the main magazine, where members share their feelings and behind-the-scenes stories about the debut single.
On top of that, the Limited Ver. comes with an exclusive, hefty 16-page mini book built around the theme "Our ROOM KEY," featuring member-color-themed room gravure shots, a "Memory ROOM" section with exclusive rare childhood photos, and imaginative talk about what it would be like to share a room with another member.
It also comes with a Limited-Ver.-only bonus: a 12-member pin-up set showing each member's "fan-service face."

Nikkei Entertainment! November 2026 Issue

Release date:October 2, 2026 (Fri)

Content:Cover & interview, special "Premium Pin-Up" bonus

KO1KEYZ takes the cover and an interview slot in Nikkei Entertainment!.
The special bonus is a "Premium Pin-Up" billed as "Right before debut! Their first-ever cover for this magazine!!" — a fitting keepsake given the timing, right on the cusp of their official debut.
The feature is tentatively titled "Talent the World Has Recognized, from Japan" (working title), so keep in mind the final title and content may still change before the issue goes on sale.

Saikyo Jump November 2026 Issue: "Saikyo Star Stream"

Release date:October 2, 2026 (Fri)

Content:First guest of new segment "Saikyo Star Stream," an 8-page feature answering 13 questions

Unlike the five fashion and entertainment magazines covered above, Saikyo Jump is an unexpected placement: a manga magazine aimed at elementary and junior high school readers.
KO1KEYZ was chosen as the very first guest for "Saikyo Star Stream," a new segment aimed at young readers who dream of becoming stars themselves, and the group put together an 8-page feature with all 12 members answering 13 questions — things like "What do you think it takes to be a star?", "What are you glad you worked hard at in elementary or middle school?", and "When did you decide on your dream for the future?"
Other questions dig into more personal territory, like "What's a slightly embarrassing story from your middle school days?" and "What did you do when you almost gave up on your dream?" — suggesting the answers will feel refreshingly down-to-earth.

It's rare for a global boy group that hasn't even debuted yet to appear in a manga magazine aimed at elementary and middle schoolers, and it's a sign of just how wide KO1KEYZ's fan base is already spreading.

S Cawaii! ME 2026 AUTUMN Standard Edition

Release date:October 5, 2026 (Mon)

Content:Cover & bundled "thick pin-up" bonus, 21-page opening feature

S Cawaii! ME 2026 AUTUMN comes in two versions — Standard and Special — and KO1KEYZ appears on the cover of the Standard Edition.
The two versions differ only in their front and back covers; the inside pages and the bundled "thick pin-up" bonus are shared between them.
The issue opens with a 21-page feature, and the Standard Edition's back cover features "#LoveSick" and P1Harmony, rounding out a star-studded lineup.

Other names appearing in this issue include Kodai Fujimoto, Hideyoshi Kan, Shin Hasegawa (THE RAMPAGE), Robin Furuya, Yuki Furukawa, Ryota Nagano, Shiki Ogawa (BUDDiiS), Riki Seto, Shu Watabe, Riyu Saito, Ayuta Fukuda (DXTEEN), Isshin Aihara (ONE LOVE ONE HEART), Eito Konishi, Motonosuke, Kosaki Koizumi, Ryoto Yonsaka, the Sato brothers, and AEN — a lineup spanning a wide range of genres.

Of all six magazines, Bessatsu Kadokawa Scene 18 offers the biggest volume of content.

Why Are So Many Magazines Lining Up for KO1KEYZ?

KO1KEYZ is a 12-member global boy group formed through "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD," set to release their debut single in October 2026.
As ViVi put it, calling them "the most-watched global boy group, summoned by ViVi faster than anywhere else in the world" — having this many women's and entertainment magazines line up covers and features around the same time, before a group has even officially debuted, is an unusual head start.
It's also notable how spread out the genres are: fashion magazines (ViVi, S Cawaii! ME), TV/entertainment magazines (Gekkan The Television, Nikkei Entertainment!), a culture magazine (Bessatsu Kadokawa Scene), and even a manga magazine for elementary and middle schoolers (Saikyo Jump) — suggesting the group is reaching a wide range of readers even before debut.

At the same time, some fans on X (formerly Twitter) have voiced frustration that, despite all these placements, the group's official account hasn't reposted any of the magazine announcements, with comments like "I've never seen an official account that doesn't promote its own magazine features" and "isn't this disrespectful to the magazines that gave them covers?"
With official posts running thin, fans have taken it upon themselves to compile magazine information on platforms like note, so expect release dates and feature details like these to keep getting updated mainly through fan-driven posts on social media.
