# Who Sings What in KO1KEYZ's "BLACK ANGEL"? The 6-Member Parts!

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the songs KO1KEYZ performed at "1ST FAN MEETING," which kicked off in August 2026, was "BLACK ANGEL -KO1KEYZ 6-member ver.-".<br>
"BLACK ANGEL" was originally performed at a concept evaluation on "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD," a dark-and-sexy track <strong>written by Hiyori Nara and composed/arranged by MADEWELL (AVEC), zeewoo (GALACTIKA*) and others &#8211; the team that performed it took first place</strong>.<br>
At the fan meeting, it was performed as a "6-member ver." by RYOGA, RYUJI, SHINHAENG, SIYOUNG, YUKI and YURA. In this article, we've mapped out the part distribution based on fans' listening notes from the performance, swapping the member emoji used in those posts for each member's romanized stage name.<br>
Note that the official audio and lyrics haven't been released yet, so the parts and lyrics below should be read as a tentative version based on fan transcription.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What is "BLACK ANGEL"? A concept evaluation song from THE NEW WORLD</h2>

<!-- wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>"BLACK ANGEL" is one of the songs performed at a concept evaluation on "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD."<br>
In that mission, the 35 trainees who survived the second rank announcement were split into five teams of seven, each performing a different original song in a different genre &#8211; and the team behind "BLACK ANGEL" placed first in that round.<br>
It was written by Hiyori Nara and composed/arranged by MADEWELL (AVEC), zeewoo (GALACTIKA*) and others, earning praise for its dark yet sexy take on a forbidden romance.<br>
For fans who followed the show, it's one of the most memorable songs from that era &#8211; the one that took first place &#8211; and at the fan meeting it was reworked as a "KO1KEYZ 6-member ver." performed by RYOGA, RYUJI, SHINHAENG, SIYOUNG, YUKI and YURA.<br>
The same fan meeting also featured another concept-evaluation song, "Neko," making the return of these show-era tracks as KO1KEYZ performances one of the highlights of the event.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The world built by "BLACK ANGEL"'s lyrics</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>True to its title, "BLACK ANGEL" is a dark, sexy song built around a <strong>forbidden, inescapable love</strong>.<br>
Angel imagery runs throughout &#8211; "the emotion I hid behind these white wings," "you, with no wings," "wings that fell can't fly anymore" &#8211; using the loss of wings as a metaphor for falling into love itself.<br>
Lines from the chorus like "if this love is a sin, I don't mind being judged for it" and "drowning in love, even to the ends of fate" convey being swallowed up by feelings there's no turning back from.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Where "Run Again" is a bright song about looking forward to the future, "BLACK ANGEL" sits at the opposite end &#8211; a more mature, decadent charm.<br>
The fact that the same group can pull off two such different worlds is likely part of why this song won first place in the concept evaluation.<br>
The "KO1KEYZ 6-member ver." performed at the fan meeting brought that show-era result back to the stage after debut, making it a performance with real sentimental weight.</p>
