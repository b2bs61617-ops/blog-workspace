"KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ," released every week on KO1KEYZ's official YouTube channel, is the "Kyuntugun Academy" series in which the members live out school life in blazer uniforms.
The classrooms, gym and schoolyard all look like a real school, so many fans have probably wondered where it was filmed.
After studying the footage closely, we found that the location is GYODA+ (Gyoda Plus), a school studio in Gyoda City, Saitama, which was formerly Gyoda Municipal Ota-Higashi Elementary School.
This article explains the three pieces of evidence behind that conclusion and walks through the places used in EP.3, "Youth Photo Contest," scene by scene.

The Location Is GYODA+ in Gyoda City, Saitama

Location: GYODA+ (Gyoda Plus)

Original school: Gyoda Municipal Ota-Higashi Elementary School (closed March 2023)

Kyuntugun Academy is set in a former elementary school surrounded by rice fields in northeastern Gyoda City, Saitama Prefecture.
It was originally Gyoda Municipal Ota-Higashi Elementary School; the building was completed in March 1984 and the school closed at the end of March 2023.
After it closed, the city chose an operator through a public call, and it is now "GYODA+," a school studio run by JELLYFISH Inc. under its "Gakusai" project and rented out for commercials, dramas and music videos.

{{IMG_exterior}}

The glass stairwell with a clock, visible behind RYUJI and YUKI as they shoot in the schoolyard, is this school's landmark.
The site has a three-story school building, a gym large enough for two basketball courts and a schoolyard of about 10,000 square meters, so an entire school can be used for filming.
The show cuts from classrooms to hallways, the gym and the schoolyard, but it was all shot on the same grounds.

How Do We Know? Three Pieces of Evidence

Evidence: a fire extinguisher sticker, the city flag in the gym, and matches with official photos

The show blurs out signs and the school flag that would reveal the school's name, and the location has not been announced.
Even so, piecing together small clues left around the footage made it possible to pin down the place clearly.

Evidence 1: The phone number on a fire extinguisher sticker

The first hint is a "fire extinguisher" sticker by a hallway window where YURA and YOSHIKI were shooting.
It appears small at the right edge of the scene where YURA sits on the window ledge, and zooming in shows the name and phone number of a local fire equipment company at the bottom.
The area code starts with "0485," a code used around Gyoda City.

{{IMG_sticker}}

Fire extinguisher inspections are usually handled by a nearby company.
So this sticker alone suggested that the school is in northern Saitama, somewhere around Gyoda.

Evidence 2: The Gyoda City flag on the gym stage

The second piece of evidence is the gym stage where KEITO and RYOGA were shooting.
Three flags hang at the back of the stage; the middle one (apparently the school flag) is blurred, but the white flag on the left is shown as is.

{{IMG_flag}}

It carries a red emblem on white: four curved wings around a four-pointed star in the center.
Comparing it with the emblems of municipalities in Saitama, it matches the emblem of Gyoda City exactly.
The red-on-white design is the same as the Gyoda City flag, and it lines up with the area code on the sticker.
A city flag in a school gym is also a sign that it was a municipal school.

Evidence 3: Five spots match the official photos

Several elementary schools in Gyoda have closed in recent years, including Kitagawara, Suka, Hoshimiya, Chuo and Ota-Higashi.
Of these, only the former Kitagawara and former Ota-Higashi schools are rented out as filming studios, so we compared their exteriors and interiors one by one.
The former Kitagawara studio's entrance and gate looked completely different, but GYODA+ at the former Ota-Higashi school matched the footage in these five spots shown on its official website.

The easiest one to spot is the gym's sawtooth roof.
The building to the left of the school in the schoolyard scenes is this gym, with the same shape as in the official exterior photo.
According to city documents, an operator was chosen in 2024 to use the former Ota-Higashi school as a filming studio and training facility, which fits the timeline.

Places Filmed in EP.3, Scene by Scene

Concept: pairs shoot with film cameras on the themes "Kyun (heart-flutter)," "Youth" and "Funny"

Places: gym, school building (entrance, stairs, classroom), schoolyard

In EP.3, six members of the photo club enter a "Youth Photo Contest."
They pick any spot in the school and take turns shooting each other with film cameras on three themes, so many parts of GYODA+ appear one after another.
The photos are set to be released on KO1KEYZ's official Instagram, and the story continues in Part 2.

Schoolyard (RYUJI & YUKI)

RYUJI and YUKI shot outdoors in the schoolyard.
Grass has grown since the school closed, which actually adds to the summery, youthful mood.

Is the EP.1 and EP.2 Classroom the Same School?

Conclusion: the EP.1 and EP.2 classroom appears to be in the same school

Evidence: the wall phone and round clock beside the blackboard, and writing on the board

The "Kyuntugun Academy" series has centered on a classroom where all 12 members gather, from EP.1 "New Student Audition" (September 17) to EP.2 "Class Trial" (September 24).
Comparing that classroom with the one in EP.3, the wall phone and round clock to the right of the blackboard are in the same place.
In EP.1 and EP.3, even the "7th" and the class-duty column written at the edge of the blackboard are identical.
The parquet floor and the shelves on the back wall also match, so the whole series appears to be filmed at the same school.

Who was found guilty in the EP.2 class trial is covered in our EP.2 class trial recap.
Next time you rewatch, keep an eye on the phone and clock in the background and you'll see it's the same room.

A Different School From "KO1KEYZ Gakuen"

KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ: a former elementary school among flat rice fields in Gyoda

KO1KEYZ Gakuen (Lemino): a different school with a hill right behind the rooftop

The show most often confused with this one is "KO1KEYZ Gakuen," the group's own program streaming on Lemino.
They are separate shows with different platforms and content; see our explainer on how "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" differs from "Gakuen" for details.

The locations differ too: the rooftop in KO1KEYZ Gakuen #0 has a green hill rising just beyond the railing, a completely different view from GYODA+ surrounded by flat fields.
We compared that rooftop in detail in our article on the rooftop that looks just like JO1's "STARGAZER" shoot.
Even within the "school" genre, each show seems to use its own school.

Can You Visit? What to Know First

Tours: a private filming studio, so no public tours or entry

Area: a quiet neighborhood of fields and homes

Knowing the location makes you want to go, but GYODA+ is a facility rented out privately for filming and training.
It does not offer public tours, so please do not enter the grounds without permission.
The school building and gym also serve as a designated local evacuation shelter.

The surrounding area is a quiet mix of fields and homes, and shoots take place on some days.
If you pass by, avoid parking on the road or making noise so that you don't disturb local residents.
Gyoda is also known for the Sakitama Kofun Cluster, Oshi Castle and as the setting of the film "The Floating Castle," so it's worth enjoying the town itself as part of a sightseeing trip.
