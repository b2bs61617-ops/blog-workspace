A Cafe Renovated from a Hanok Built in 1966

ium1966 is a cafe opened in May 2024 after an owner with a passion for vintage items spent 3 years renovating a Hanok (traditional Korean house) built in 1966.
The "1966" in the store's name comes from the year the building was originally constructed.
Rooms are arranged around a central courtyard, offering table seating as well as traditional floor-seating rooms where you take off your shoes to enter.

The room where the 5 members sat was a floor-seating room decorated with wooden lattice doors and vintage chests.
Sitting on the floor around low wooden dining tables, the members got excited as soon as they walked in, saying, "This is so stylish!"
Even though it's just a step away from the bustling streets of Hongdae, it feels like a quiet, cozy space where you're visiting an old home.

ium1966 Menu & Prices

Here is a summary of ium1966's menu and prices listed on the Korean food website "Diningcode".
Menu items featuring fruit may change in ingredients and prices depending on the season.

The "black sesame" someone read out from the menu is believed to be the Black Sesame Cream Latte (흑임자 크림 라떼).

Tips & Things to Keep in Mind for Pilgrimage Visits

Check Before You Go

  • Remove shoes before entering the floor-seating room
  • As it's an authentic old Hanok, there are high thresholds and steps between rooms
  • Fruit menu items change according to the season
  • Check Instagram for holidays/closures

Floor-seating rooms like the one the 5 members sat in require removing your shoes.
Because it preserves the original Hanok structure, the thresholds at room entrances are quite high, and Korean reviews note that "you need to be a little careful when walking around carrying drinks."
When carrying your order on a tray, make sure to watch your step!

Whether you can sit in the exact floor-seating room as the members depends on how crowded it is that day.
Since the cafe is a place for other customers to relax as well, please be respectful of cafe etiquette—such as making sure not to include other people in your photos.
Hongdae also features the Korean BBQ restaurant and self-photo studio from the same Vlog, so making a full tour of all the spots is highly recommended!
