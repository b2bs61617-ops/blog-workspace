A post from KO1KEYZ's official X account has sparked confusion online, as fans mix up two similarly named new shows: "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" and "KO1KEYZ Gakuen."
To be clear: these are two completely different shows.
"KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" is a free weekly series launching on the official YouTube channel at 21:00 (JST) on September 17, 2026, while "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" is KO1KEYZ's first-ever flagship program, streaming exclusively on Lemino starting October 1 — the platform, pricing, and start dates are all different.
This article breaks down both shows so you can tell them apart.

  • When and where "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" streams
  • When and where "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" streams
  • Why fans are confusing the two shows
  • How to tell them apart

Why Are the Two Shows Being Confused?

With KO1KEYZ's debut set for October 7, 2026, announcements have been coming one after another throughout September.
Following the "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" announcement, the similarly named "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" was revealed shortly after, leading some fans on X to wonder whether the two were actually the same show.
The similar names, the fact that both are framed as "KO1KEYZ's first ___," and start dates just a week apart (September 17 and September 24) have all added to the confusion.
Some posts on X even went out of their way to clarify that the two are, in fact, separate programs.

What Is "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ"?

Start date:September 17, 2026 (Thu) 21:00 JST

Platform:KO1KEYZ Official YouTube channel

Frequency / price:Weekly on Thursdays, free

"KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" is a new series streaming on KO1KEYZ's official YouTube channel.
KO1KEYZ's official X account announced that new episodes will drop "every Thursday," and unlike a service like TVer or Lemino, it's free to watch for anyone directly on the official YouTube channel.
The launch comes with fewer than 20 days left until the October 7 debut, and fans have been reading it as part of a countdown push toward the group's debut.
Details of the first episode haven't been revealed yet, but the announcement post used a clip of member KOSUKE smiling and waving, suggesting the members themselves will appear in the show.
Around the same time, KO1KEYZ's official account also announced that the debut single's music video had surpassed 6 million views, suggesting late September is an especially active stretch for the group on YouTube.

What Is "KO1KEYZ Gakuen"?

Platform:Lemino (exclusive)

Start date:#0 = Sept 24, 2026 (Thu) 13:00 (free), #1 = Oct 1 (Thu) 19:00 (Lemino Premium)

Content:All 12 members become "class presidents" tackling special school-themed challenges

"KO1KEYZ Gakuen" is KO1KEYZ's first flagship program, streaming as the third installment of the LAPONE x Lemino collaboration slot.
Each of the 12 members is assigned the role of "class president," and the show follows them as they throw themselves into a series of special lessons designed to deepen the group's bond, in a school-themed variety format.
Episode #0, offered for free, streams from 13:00 on September 24 (Thu), while the main series' episode #1 streams from 19:00 on October 1 (Thu) exclusively for Lemino Premium (paid) subscribers; episode #2, on October 8 (Thu) at 19:00, has already been announced as well.
In short, while "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" is a free YouTube show airing weekly at 21:00 on Thursdays, "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" is a Lemino-exclusive flagship program built around a paid subscription model.
For more on how "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" was announced and how to watch it, see our article on KO1KEYZ Gakuen's Lemino streaming announcement.

Summary: How to Tell the Two Shows Apart

✓"KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" = KO1KEYZ's official YouTube, starting Sept 17 (Thu) 21:00, weekly, free

✓"KO1KEYZ Gakuen" = Lemino-exclusive flagship program, #0 free on Sept 24 (Thu), #1 onward requires Lemino Premium from Oct 1 (Thu)

Keep an eye on the platform and airtime for each so you don't miss either of the two new shows.
