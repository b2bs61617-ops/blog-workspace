<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Tips Before Copying KEITO's Look</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div style="border:1px solid #ddd;border-left:4px solid #f0a35a;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 16px;margin:0 0 16px 0;background:#fdf6ee;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Key point:</strong>If it's your first time, get an eye exam and match the BC and power</p>
</div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Colored contacts are a fun way to change your look, but they're also medical devices that sit directly on your eyes.<br>
If you're new to them or haven't seen an eye doctor in a while, it's a good idea to have your base curve (BC) and prescription checked before buying.<br>
Forcing yourself to wear lenses that don't fit can lead to redness or dryness.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Also, the same lens can look quite different depending on your natural eye color and the lighting.<br>
KEITO's video was filmed under bright indoor lights, which may have made the gray translucency stand out more than usual.<br>
Rather than trying to recreate his exact look, choosing a shade that suits your own eyes is the safer approach.</p>
