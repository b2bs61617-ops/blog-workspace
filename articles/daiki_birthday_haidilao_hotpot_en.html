When did DAIKI get his early birthday celebration?

Date: October 2, 2026

Shared by DAIKI on: the fan messaging service "Private Chat"

News of the celebration came on October 2, 2026.
That day, DAIKI sent a selfie to the fan messaging service "Private Chat," holding a light-up birthday board with both hands.
The board was lit with the Haidilao logo and the words "Happy birthday" written in Japanese.
Along with the photo, he thanked the friends who had celebrated his birthday, so it looks like they had planned the celebration for him.

DAIKI was born on October 8, 2004, and turns 22 this year.
It will be his first birthday since debuting with KO1KEYZ, so the celebrations from the group and fans on the day itself should be lively.
Getting to spend relaxed time with longtime friends before the big day must have made him happy too.

Because Private Chat is a paid service, we have chosen not to show the photo in this article.

Where did they celebrate? Haidilao Hot Pot

Restaurant: Haidilao Hot Pot

Branch: likely in Japan, but not disclosed

The celebration took place at Haidilao, a hot pot chain from Sichuan, China.
That same night, GOTEN also posted a Haidilao photo to his Instagram Story.
The table is packed with beef laid out on a round board, pork belly and tofu-skin rolls, and three hands are raising logo-printed drinks in a toast.
The only caption was a short "Let's all do our best."

The branch hasn't been named, but since the birthday board DAIKI held was written in Japanese, it was most likely a branch in Japan.
Haidilao also operates in Korea and elsewhere, so the Japanese board is a big clue for narrowing down the location.

What kind of restaurant is Haidilao?

Haidilao is a hot pot chain founded in 1994 in Sichuan, China.
It runs more than 1,500 restaurants worldwide, and you can compare several broths, centered on authentic mala and including bone broth and tomato, in a divided pot.
Shows such as Sichuan face-changing and staff stretching noodles right at your table are a big part of its appeal.
As DAIKI experienced, staff may also celebrate birthday guests with a light-up birthday board.

Many branches also stay open late; in Japan, for example, the Ikebukuro branch is open from 11 a.m. to 6 a.m. and the Shinjuku branch from 11 a.m. to 7 a.m.
That makes it an easy place to drop by after a long day, and a good fit for a meal with friends squeezed into a busy schedule.

Haidilao branches in Japan (as listed on the official Japanese site)

  • Tokyo: Ikebukuro, Shinjuku, Akihabara, Ueno
  • Kanagawa: Yokohama Station, Kawasaki
  • Chiba: Kaihin-Makuhari
  • Osaka: Namba
  • Hokkaido: Niseko (winter only)

If you'd like to order the same drinks at the same chain as the three of them, check the official Haidilao Japan website for the branch nearest you.

Who was with him? Most likely GOTEN and I.DAIKI

Likely companions: GOTEN (Goten Kurahashi), I.DAIKI (Daiki Ichikawa)

Official announcement: none

DAIKI and GOTEN were at the same hot pot chain on the same day, and the toast had exactly three hands.
That naturally drew attention to who the third person was.
At first, fans hoped it was KINARI (Kinari Kenmotsu), who had worn matching bracelets with DAIKI and GOTEN, but the view that "it was I.DAIKI (Daiki Ichikawa)" soon spread.
That said, none of the three has announced that they went together, and only hands appear in the photo.

GOTEN (Goten Kurahashi)

Born November 5, 2004, from Aichi Prefecture, and 172 cm tall.
After appearing on LDH's dance battle audition, he took on Nippu Shinsekai and made it to the 22 finalists.
He didn't make KO1KEYZ's final 12, but he and DAIKI have stayed close enough after the show to wear matching bracelets.

I.DAIKI (Daiki Ichikawa)

Born December 1, 2004, from Aichi Prefecture, and 183 cm tall.
On Nippu Shinsekai he was listed as "I.DAIKI" to tell him apart from DAIKI (Daiki Kato), who shares the same first name.
Japanese fans call him "Ai-Daiki," and he loves beauty, listing makeup and skincare among his hobbies.
He went from trainee candidate to official trainee through the "101 PASS," but placed 66th at the first ranking announcement and left the show.

What do the three have in common? The 2004-born Aichi crew

In common: all born in 2004, all from Aichi Prefecture, all former Nippu Shinsekai trainees

Put their profiles side by side and an unexpected connection appears.
DAIKI, GOTEN and I.DAIKI were all born in 2004, and all three come from Aichi Prefecture.
Their birthdays even fall one month apart in October, November and December, and Japanese fans call them the "Aichi crew."

On the show, the three went separate ways: a debut, a finalist spot, and an exit at the first ranking announcement.
Still, being the same age from the same hometown probably makes it easy for them to get together outside the show.
Knowing that, GOTEN's short "Let's all do our best" reads like three friends cheering each other on as they chase their dreams in different places.
