EP.4 of KO1KEYZ's official YouTube variety show "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" was released on Thursday, October 22, 2026.
This episode kicked off Part 1 of "Kyuntugun Academy: After-School Tag & Hide," featuring all 12 members taking over the entire school after classes for an epic game of hide-and-tag.
To cut straight to the chase: the two taggers ("it") were YOSHIKI and RYOGA, while the remaining 10 members scattered across the nurse's office, the teachers' office, the bushes by the school gate, behind the gym, and beyond.
Part 1 wrapped up right at the end of the 10-minute hiding phase, cutting off with a "To be continued" just as the school chime rang and the taggers sprang into action.
In this article, we've put together a full recap covering the game rules, the tagger duo's strategy meeting, and each member's hiding spot!

What was EP.4's "After-School Tag & Hide" all about?

The scene opens in their familiar classroom, "Class 1-Kyun," with an on-screen caption reading "After School."
With classes over and plenty of free time left, RYUJI sparked the idea: "Right now, I'm really feeling some hide-and-tag..." followed by, "Why don't we use the entire school to play hide-and-tag?"
The other members quickly chimed in with "That's so nostalgic!" and "Now I really want to do it!", getting everyone instantly on board.
Decked out in matching tracksuits and each armed with a handheld camera, all 12 members sprinted through the hallways and across the schoolyard, making this one of their most physically intense episodes yet!

{{IMG_rules}}

Hide-and-Tag Rules

  1. Start with 2 taggers ("it")
  2. Caught runners turn into taggers (infection-style tag: the tagger team keeps growing)
  3. Hiding time: 10 minutes
  4. Runners win if they survive for 30 minutes

While discussing the tagger setup—DAIKI noted, "One person is definitely too few," and RYUJI asked, "How about three?"—someone suggested, "Why not just let the taggers multiply?" That settled it: they'd start with two taggers, and anyone caught would join the tagger team in a classic "infection tag" format.
They also made sure to confirm the boundaries on the spot: off-limits areas were strictly forbidden, while the gymnasium and outdoor grounds were fully fair game.
An on-screen disclaimer also noted: "Filming was conducted in a privately rented facility with full permission granted for all areas used."

YOSHIKI and RYOGA are "It"! How the taggers were chosen

When asked, "Who wants to be 'it'?", nobody raised their hands right away, with SIYOUNG stating flatly, "I want to run."
Stepping up as MC, KOSUKE took control: "The three who haven't raised their hands will do rock-paper-scissors," getting things moving.
RYOGA then murmured, "I wouldn't mind doing it," prompting DAIKI to instantly react: "Wait! Isn't he super fast?!" and ISSA to add, "RYOGA is genuinely terrifying."
YOSHIKI chipped in with, "Wait, isn't YURA track and field too?", to which KOSUKE quipped, "Two track guys together is way too scary!"—making running speed the ultimate talking point.
In the end, RYOGA accepted the role with an "Alright, I'll do it," teaming up with YOSHIKI as the starting taggers.
On screen, captions labeled them the "Pink Oni (Tagger)" for YOSHIKI and the "Navy Oni (Tagger)" for RYOGA.

{{IMG_oni}}

While everyone else went off to hide, the tagger duo waited in a second-floor classroom.
YOSHIKI hyped himself up, saying, "Gotta tie my shoelaces tight. I'm literally going all-out sprinting today," and RYOGA agreed, "Same here, obviously."
When RYOGA asked, "YOSHIKI, were you a sprinter?", YOSHIKI revealed that in middle school he was a track sprinter, while in high school he was primarily in the light music club but split his time doing long-distance track as well.
During their strategy meeting, RYOGA suggested, "Let's first catch one person together as a pair," while YOSHIKI analyzed the building: "I want to clear this school building first. They're probably not on this floor anyway."
YOSHIKI clearly grasped the meta of infection tag, noting: "It all comes down to how fast we can bag that very first person. Once we get one, it'll snowball from there."
Toward the end of the wait time, the two even started stretching and doing warm-up exercises—showing just how seriously they were taking the game!

KEITO: The master strategist setting traps in the nurse's office

KEITO was easily putting his brain to work the most right from the get-go.
Muttering, "Since it's hide-and-tag, hiding somewhere surprisingly close might actually be a great move," he stepped into the 1st-floor nurse's office and declared: "I want to throw them off, so I'm gonna set a little trap here in the nurse's office. I'm a master strategist, after all—gotta buy us some time."
Right on cue, the caption "Master Strategist KEITO" flashed on screen.

{{IMG_keito}}

After that, KEITO scouted outside, remarking, "This spot actually has surprisingly clear visibility," before hurrying back inside as panic set in: "Wait, 10 minutes is way shorter than I thought!"
He ended up on the 3rd floor, showing off his strategic mindset once again: "We start from the 2nd floor, but psychologically, everyone wants to go downward. Going up might actually be the ultimate blind spot."
However, upon arriving at the 3rd floor, he ran straight into KOSUKE: "Someone had the exact same idea!" When KOSUKE warned him, "Bro, there is literally nothing on the 3rd floor," KEITO hilariously had to turn back: "Welp, that failed."
What exact "trap" he set in the nurse's office was left a mystery in Part 1!

TOWA: From fooling around in the schoolyard to hiding in an office cabinet

TOWA dashed off full of energy: "I'm running! No way I'm getting caught!"
At first, he headed out to the schoolyard with SIYOUNG, asking, "Going outside is allowed, right?" But the second he spotted a soccer ball, he went full distracted mode: "Oh! A goal?!" and started playing soccer!
Unfortunately for him, tagger YOSHIKI was watching the entire scene from the 2nd-floor classroom window, noting: "Yup, yup, yup, so that's where you went," as the caption read: "Two people playing in the schoolyard instead of hiding."
Realizing the tagger was watching, TOWA gasped, "The tagger is looking right at us!" and scrambled back inside: "Never mind, inside it is. Outside is way too dangerous. Total waste of time!"

{{IMG_towa}}

Dodging off-limits rooms along the way, he eventually slipped into the 1st-floor teachers' office.
Just as he was trying to squeeze into a storage cabinet asking, "Can I hide in here?", KOSUKE walked in, creating a funny exchange: "I kind of want to hide together, but... that looks kinda suspicious, honestly."
Struggling to pull the doors shut—"I can't close it!" "Just use momentum!" "Scary, feels like I'm gonna pinch my fingers!"—TOWA finally managed to pull it off: "TOWA infiltration complete."
Sitting inside the pitch-black cabinet, he mumbled, "It's so hot... I have to stay here for 20 minutes? What do I do?"
Hilariously enough, YUKI peeked into the teachers' office shortly after, decided "Nah, this room looks bad," and walked away—completely missing TOWA hiding inside!

ISSA and YURA: The outdoor bush hiders

ISSA started out with supreme confidence, smiling: "Everyone else is in full panic mode, but look at me, totally chill."
While heading outside with YURA, RYUJI, and KOSUKE, he pointed out the harsh reality of infection tag: "Once the taggers start multiplying, you can't trust a single soul, right? This is basically an every-man-for-himself free-for-all."
His final chosen spot was a bush right beside the school gate near the Kinjiro Ninomiya statue.
"Near this school gate... I can sprint away from this side OR that side, so I'm picking here," he reasoned, smartly planning his escape routes in advance—earning him the on-screen nickname "Bush-ISSA."

{{IMG_issa}}

On the flip side, YURA was firmly on Team Outdoors from the start, insisting, "Just constantly sprinting around the schoolyard is honestly the easiest way."
Even when ISSA cautioned him, "Yeah, but if those two corner you, isn't it game over?", YURA confidently replied, "I trust my speed"—prompting the caption: "YURA, confident in his running speed."
He ultimately tucked himself behind the trees at the back of the gym, softly commentating: "Wait, I was so desperate to hide that I haven't spoken a single word... Right now, I'm blending into the trees behind the gym. I have zero presence anyway, so fingers crossed they don't spot me."
At one point, ISSA even spotted him from his bush: "Wait, YURA is right there!", revealing that the two outdoor runners ended up hiding surprisingly close to each other.

{{IMG_yura}}

RYUJI, KOSUKE, and DAIKI: Ending up together in the nurse's office

The trio of RYUJI, KOSUKE, and DAIKI (joined by YURA initially) started off by scouting the building thoroughly.
They found a promising spot behind the building, with DAIKI noting, "We should figure out which areas are off-limits first," "Oh, there's stairs here," and "Didn't we basically just win?", while RYUJI agreed: "There's no way they find us here!"
However, indecision struck when DAIKI admitted, "Part of me kinda wants to get found just so I can sprint for my life," and KOSUKE added, "I really want to lose them outdoors."

As they kept searching for spots, RYUJI wanted to go higher up ("I really want to go all the way to the top!"). Meanwhile, in the 1st-floor principal's office, DAIKI started freaking out: "It's so creepy! Being in here alone is terrifying! No way, absolutely hate being in a place like this!"
KOSUKE made his way up to the 3rd floor, checking out the area—"We can access the rooftop, but..."—before admitting, "Wait, this might actually be genuinely scary."
In the end, all three wandered back down to the 1st-floor nurse's office—the very room KEITO had supposedly tampered with earlier!
RYUJI spotted a cabinet and suggested, "How about this nurse's office? There's a chance this spot actually works." But just as he hesitated—"Wait, won't they definitely open this cabinet?"—and KOSUKE agreed—"They will... yeah, they definitely will open it"—the bell suddenly tolled!

What about SHINHAENG, YUKI, and SIYOUNG?

SHINHAENG made his entrance when YUKI, who was scouting the gymnasium, suddenly spotted him with a caption: "SHINHAENG-hyung spotted!"
SHINHAENG asked, "How's this spot?", to which YUKI reacted: "What on earth are you doing up there? I mean, I guess it works, but..."
Later, SHINHAENG managed to climb up to an elevated structure near the gym, explaining to his camera, "I came from over there."

YUKI was easily the member who struggled the most to settle on a hiding spot.
Deep inside the gym storage, he found a potential hiding place—"Red cloth here, this works. Keeping it as a candidate for now"—but couldn't commit. In the teachers' office, he sighed, "Nah, this room looks bad... probably about 5 minutes left."
Toward the end, he slipped into a dark room: "I'll hide here. There's no lights." But mere seconds later, he realized: "Wait, this area is off-limits! Shoot!" and darted back out, cutting his screen time short. His final location will be revealed in Part 2!

As for SIYOUNG, after playing around in the schoolyard with TOWA, his location remained completely masked as "1F ???" on screen.
Just as he began to say, "I'm currently...", the screen cut him off with the teaser caption: "Just where on earth is SIYOUNG?!", leaving his hiding spot as a total mystery for next week.
Seeing where SIYOUNG—who boldly announced "I want to run" at the very beginning—chose to bunker down will definitely be one of the highlights to watch for!

Where is the school filming location?

EP.4 appears to have been filmed at the same school location used in previous episodes.
The filming site from EP.3 was identified as "Gyoda Plus (GYODA+)," a school studio located in Gyoda City, Saitama Prefecture (formerly Gyoda Municipal Ota Higashi Elementary School). The wooden-walled gymnasium draped with green curtains perfectly matches the scenery in this episode.
Furthermore, Gyoda Plus's official website lists the nurse's office, teachers' office, principal's office, music room, gym, and schoolyard as available filming areas—lining up identically with all the rooms the members scouted and hid in.
While the illustrated map caption also featured a swimming pool, the facility's official site lists the pool as "Unavailable for use."

Highlights to look out for in EP.4

Without a doubt, the biggest charm of this episode is how everyone's true personality shone through their hiding choices.
From KEITO laying down mind games in the nurse's office before heading upstairs, to ISSA picking a bush with multiple getaway routes, YURA banking on his "zero presence" behind the trees, and DAIKI, RYUJI and KOSUKE getting spooked as they searched together—each member spent their 10 minutes in totally contrasting ways!
The moment YOSHIKI caught TOWA and SIYOUNG casually kicking a ball around the schoolyard from his window was also pure comedic gold, and likely a huge foreshadowing moment for Part 2.

Another highlight is just how insanely locked-in the tagger duo is.
With YOSHIKI's track background and RYOGA's infamous sprint speed, seeing them methodically plan out their hunt ("Getting that first person is key") before breaking into full pre-game stretches made KOSUKE's early fear—"Two track guys together is way too scary"—feel terrifyingly real.
Part 1 signed off with the chilling caption: "The Pink Oni and Navy Oni finally make their move." Who will be their very first victim? Will anyone actually manage to survive all 30 minutes? We'll find out in Part 2!
