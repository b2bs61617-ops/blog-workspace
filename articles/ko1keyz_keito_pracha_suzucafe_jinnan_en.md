# [KEITO] Which Shibuya Cafe Did He Recommend on Pracha?

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>KO1KEYZ's KEITO (Keito Ono) is believed to have recommended a cafe on the fan-only chat "KO1KEYZ Chat (Pracha)" — and it turned out to be "SUZU CAFE Jinnan" in Shibuya's Jinnan district.<br>
Known for its antique furniture, elegant interior, and homemade desserts like a blueberry rare cheesecake made with brown sugar, this <strong>long-loved cafe sits within walking distance of Shibuya Station, in Jinnan 1-chome</strong>.<br>
This article covers what kind of place SUZU CAFE Jinnan is, the menu items KEITO is thought to have recommended, and KEITO's reputation on Pracha.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Cafe KEITO Recommended on Pracha: "SUZU CAFE Jinnan"</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div style="border:1px solid #f2ddc4;border-left:4px solid #e0812f;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 16px;margin:0 0 16px 0;background:#fdf6ee;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Name:</strong>SUZU CAFE Jinnan<br>
<strong>Access:</strong>A few minutes' walk from JR/Tokyo Metro Shibuya Station<br>
<strong>Atmosphere:</strong>Elegant interior with antique sofas and designer furniture; dishes made with organic vegetables and naturally leavened bread</p>
</div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>"SUZU CAFE Jinnan," located on the 3rd floor of the "VORT Shibuya briller" building in Shibuya's Jinnan district, has come up as the cafe KEITO recommended on Pracha.<br>
Pracha (KO1KEYZ Chat) is a monthly subscription service that lets KO1KEYZ members send messages and photos directly to subscribed fans, and KEITO appears to have mentioned this cafe through it.<br>
Despite its convenient location within walking distance of Shibuya Station, the cafe offers a calm space filled with antique sofas and designer furniture, serving dishes made with organic vegetables and naturally leavened bread — a spot that's been loved locally for years.</p>

<p>Soft lighting and greenery fill the space inside, giving it a relaxed cafe feel during the day and a dining atmosphere for grilled dishes and pasta at night — its mood shifts depending on the time of visit.<br>
Review sites put its rating at around 3.6 (from over 500 reviews), and the menu also includes a solid lineup of meals, such as the monthly "SUZU Plate" for 1,680 yen (tax included).</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Desserts KEITO Is Thought to Have Recommended</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Among SUZU CAFE Jinnan's dessert menu, these three items stand out in particular.<br>
The <strong><span class="swl-marker mark_orange">Vanilla Ice Cream &amp; Custard Pudding</span></strong> tops the cafe's signature firm-set custard pudding — made with a Vietnamese-style caramel hiding a touch of coffee — with vanilla ice cream, making it a step up from the standalone custard pudding (750 yen).<br>
The Velvet Gateau au Chocolat is known for its rich, melt-in-the-mouth dark chocolate, while the Blueberry Rare Cheesecake made with brown sugar balances a refreshing tartness with gentle sweetness.</p>

<p>All three desserts get a 50-yen discount with a drink set between 2pm and 6pm (excluding weekends and holidays), so fans hoping to try the same menu as KEITO might want to stop by on a weekday afternoon.<br>
