On October 5, 2026, two days before the release of their debut single "KO1KEYZ," KO1KEYZ put up large-scale station ads in Tokyo and Osaka.
The locations are "Big 20" at Tokyu Toyoko Line Shibuya Station and "D-St. (D-Street)" ①–④ at Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station, and they will be up for one week, until Sunday, October 11.
Both spots are a little tricky to find inside the stations, so this article covers how to get there from the ticket gates, how the Shibuya and Osaka designs differ, and the CD shop displays you can visit nearby.

Where Are the KO1KEYZ Ads? Two Spots in Tokyo and Osaka

Tokyo: "Big 20," Tokyu Toyoko Line Shibuya Station

Osaka: "D-St. (D-Street)" ①–④, Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station

Dates: Oct 5 (Mon) – Oct 11 (Sun)

The ads were announced on the morning of October 5 on the official X account and in the NEWS section of the official website.
The announcement image is titled "KO1KEYZ: VISIT LIST," the same series as the member solo screen ads that ran at 10 stations in Japan and Korea in September.
The solo screens ended on October 4 and the large ads started the very next day, so the campaign has effectively passed the baton into debut week.

{{IMG_official}}

The ads run for exactly one week, from Monday, October 5 to Sunday, October 11.
Since the release day, Wednesday, October 7, falls in the middle, many fans will probably stop by on their way to buy the CD.
Both ads include a QR code labeled "Music Video," so you can scan it on the spot and go straight to the MV.

If you want to know which member appeared at which station in September, see our guide to the member solo screen stations.

Shibuya Station Big 20: Location and Directions

Location: Outside the "Miyamasuzaka East Gate" (宮益坂東改札) of the Tokyu Toyoko / Tokyo Metro Fukutoshin Line Shibuya Station (B2F)

Landmark: Toward Exit B1

Design: Solo shots of all 12 members + the jacket photo

"Big 20" is a large poster ad space in Shibuya Station on the Tokyu Toyoko Line and Tokyo Metro Fukutoshin Line.
The name comes from its 20 B0-size posters (about 1.46 m wide × 1.03 m tall each) arranged 10 across and 2 high, making the whole wall roughly 14.6 m wide and 2.1 m tall.
The space opened in 2020 and sits just outside the ticket gate closest to MIYASHITA PARK.

The key point is to exit through the Miyamasuzaka East Gate.
Shibuya Station has many gates underground, and leaving through a different one means a long walk to the ad.
The Miyamasuzaka East Gate is on B2F; once you're out, follow the signs for "Exit B1" and take the escalator down, and all 12 members line the wall right beside you.
If you arrive on the Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line or Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line, just follow the station signs to the Miyamasuzaka East Gate and take the same route.
Coming from the JR or Ginza Line, head underground first and look for signs to "Exit B1" so you don't get lost.

{{IMG_shibuya}}

The Shibuya ad shows solo shots of the 12 members in a single row, with the debut single's jacket photo, logo and MV QR code on the far right.
Each member gets a large vertical panel, which makes it easy to stand in front of your bias for a photo, something unique to Shibuya.
Plenty of fans have already posted photos holding acrylic stands or photocards up in front of the ad.

Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station D-St.: Location and Directions

Location: "D Passage" on the 1st floor of Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station (outside the gates)

Landmark: The passage from JR Osaka Station toward HEP FIVE / HEP NAVIO

Design: Three 4-member panels + one group photo panel

"D-St. (D-Street)" is a poster ad space on the walls of the "D Passage," which runs under the JR tracks on the 1st floor of Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station.
Of the passages connecting the Hankyu Umeda Main Store and Hankyu Osaka-umeda Station, look for the one at the far end on the HEP FIVE side.
It's outside the ticket gates, so you don't need to buy a ticket or platform pass to see it.

From JR Osaka Station, walk toward Hankyu and take the passage heading in the direction of HEP FIVE and HEP NAVIO, and the ads will come into view.
The area around Umeda is so confusing it's nicknamed the "Umeda Dungeon," so it helps to check where "D Passage" is on the Osaka-umeda Station map on Hankyu's official website first.
The passage with the ads is long and straight, so once you're there you can simply walk along it from one end.

{{IMG_umeda_corridor}}

D-St. is an ad space where each spot holds B0 posters 4 across and 2 high, with up to 4 spots available.
The announcement says "①–④," which means all 4 spots, 32 posters in total, are filled with KO1KEYZ.
Unlike Shibuya, the Osaka ads consist of three panels with 4 members each and one panel with the 12-member group photo plus the jacket photo.

Each 4-member panel has the "KO1KEYZ" logo and "DEBUT SINGLE 2026.10.07 RELEASE" along the bottom band, with the MV QR code in the lower right.
The group panel shows all 12 members standing in a row against a futuristic building, just like the debut single's jacket.
Check the Osaka section of the official announcement image beforehand to see which panel your bias is on, and they'll be easier to find on site.

How Do the Shibuya and Osaka Ads Differ?

Shibuya: 12 solo shots side by side on one wall

Osaka: Split into 4 panels: 4 members × 3 + a group photo

Both ads use the same debut single visuals but present them in clearly different ways.
Here's a table to compare them at a glance.

In Shibuya, all 12 members are on one long wall, so to fit everyone in one shot, step back a little and shoot at an angle.
In Osaka, the panels are separate, so shooting each one straight on gives you all 12 members and the group photo in four pictures.
If you can make it to both Tokyo and Osaka, comparing the two sets of photos side by side could be fun too.

Things to Keep in Mind When Visiting

The official announcement also includes some guidelines for fans going to see the ads.
Since these ads are in busy station passages, please keep the following in mind.

Tips for Visiting the Ads

  1. Don't contact the stations, station staff or nearby facilities with questions
  2. If crowds cause congestion, the ads may be taken down early
  3. Be considerate of other passengers when viewing and taking photos
  4. Share your photos on social media with "#KO1KEYZに会いに恋"

Both passages get crowded during the morning and evening rush hours.
Fans who visited Umeda on the morning of the first day said there were few people around and they could photograph every member cleanly.
If you want to take your time, weekday afternoons and other off-peak hours are your best bet.

Which CD Shops Nearby Can You Visit Too?

Shibuya: Tower Records Shibuya, HMV&BOOKS SHIBUYA

Umeda: Tower Records Umeda NU Chayamachi, HMV Grand Front Osaka

On the same day as the ads, October 5, it was also announced that shops selected through the CD shop pre-order campaign will host a "limited special display."
The display includes autographed boards with messages from the members, a message video from all 12 members, and a wide-format group instax photo.
The wide instax photo isn't just on display: it will be given away by lottery to people who buy the debut single at that shop.

Of the 21 shops selected nationwide, the ones in Shibuya are Tower Records Shibuya and HMV&BOOKS SHIBUYA, and the ones in Umeda are Tower Records Umeda NU Chayamachi and HMV Grand Front Osaka.
All of them are within walking distance of the stations with the ads, making it easy to plan a route from the ad to the CD shop.
If you go on release day, October 7, you can enjoy the ad, the display and buying the CD all in one day.
