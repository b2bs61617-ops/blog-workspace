Part 2 of EP.4 of KO1KEYZ's official YouTube show, "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ," was released on Thursday, November 5, 2026.
Picking up right where Part 1 left off—after all 12 members finished hiding and the chime rang—the taggers finally unleash chaos across the school building and the schoolyard.
To cut right to the chase, the only two who managed to survive until the very end were YURA and SIYOUNG.
YURA hid inside the trees behind the gym, while SIYOUNG hid in the room across from the broadcasting room on the 1st floor. However, suspicions have arisen that SIYOUNG's hiding spot might have actually been "off-limits"!
In this article, we'll break down the order and locations of who got caught, TOWA's hilarious "I didn't get tagged!" drama, and the post-game reveal.

EP.4 Part 2 Synopsis

Continuing from EP.3, the setting is the school campus, where all 12 members clad in tracksuits race through the hallways and the schoolyard holding action cameras for the second half of "After-School Hide-and-Tag."
The rules remain the same as Part 1: starting with YOSHIKI and RYOGA as the original taggers, anyone caught instantly joins the tagger side, multiplying their numbers in classic zombie-tag style.
On-screen captions referred to the taggers by their member colors—like "Light Blue Oni" or "Yellow-Green Oni"—and a chalkboard-style "Taggers vs. Runners" board regularly flashed on screen to keep track of the progress.

Part 2 also reveals where YUKI and SIYOUNG ended up after failing to find a spot before the end of Part 1, as well as the fate of KEITO and his crew after setting up their "trap" in the nurse's office.
Check out our EP.4 Part 1 recap article for all the initial hiding spots!

Order and Locations of Eliminations

Here is the complete list of who was caught, in order, along with their locations.
The location names match the on-screen captions shown in the top-left corner.

The first half saw members hiding outdoors get picked off one after another, while the second half shifted indoors as the school building was combed through.
Let's dive into the highlights in order of elimination!

1st to Go: ISSA! The Tagger Duo's Flawless Teamwork

As the chime rang, the two taggers started by checking inside the school building.
YOSHIKI suggested, "Maybe we should check the gym once," while RYOGA seemed slightly flustered by the initial lack of targets, saying, "Seriously? There's really no one around."
With the caption reading "Pink Oni & Ultramarine Oni fired up to catch them for real," they sprinted around hunting for their very first target, staying true to their Part 1 strategy: "The first catch is everything."

Meanwhile, ISSA—who had been hiding in the bushes near the school gate—decided not to just sit still and boldly went on the move.
The caption joked, "Making a move-ssa," keeping up the ongoing pun on his name from Part 1 ("Bush-ssa," "Decisive-ssa").
However, RYOGA, searching behind the school building alone, spotted him: "Ultramarine Oni spots ISSA!"
RYOGA yelled out, "Found ISSA in the yard!" and "He's heading toward the gym!", allowing YOSHIKI to cut him off and corner ISSA together.

{{IMG_issa}}

The replay caption praised them with "ISSA secured through brilliant teamwork," showing off how smoothly the duo executed their pincer plan.
ISSA was quickly turned into a tagger, bringing the tagger count to three.

3 Caught at Once Outside the Gym!

The next wave of action unfolded around the gym.
KOSUKE and DAIKI, who had originally been in the nurse's office in Part 1, were now outside near the gym. Sensing danger, the caption popped up: "KOSUKE and DAIKI sense something's wrong."
Meanwhile, RYOGA was lying in wait around the corner of the gym as "Ultramarine Oni quietly lying in ambush," ready to pounce on fleeing runners.
KEITO was also hiding outside the gym, nervously muttering, "Heart's pounding..."

Things escalated in an instant, and DAIKI and KEITO were captured simultaneously.
Despite boasting "I'm a master strategist" after rigging his trap in the nurse's office, KEITO ended up getting caught outside, leaving his trap completely untested!

{{IMG_gym}}

Amid all the chaos, KOSUKE kept running for his life.
With the caption "KOSUKE keeps running amid the chaos," he made a wide loop around the schoolyard. But newly minted tagger ISSA alerted the others: "Behind the gym, behind the gym!" and proposed, "Let's flank him!"
KEITO chimed in, "Pinching KOSUKE," leading to the caption: "Taggers zero in on KOSUKE."
In the end, KOSUKE cried out, "There's no way!" as he was tagged.

{{IMG_kosuke}}

As ISSA chased down KOSUKE, a hilarious caption read: "Light Blue Oni, showing zero traces of being a runner just minutes ago"—his immediate, full-throttle switch to tagger mode had everyone laughing.
At this point, the taggers numbered six (RYOGA, YOSHIKI, ISSA, DAIKI, KEITO, and KOSUKE), while the remaining runners also stood at six (RYUJI, SHINHAENG, SIYOUNG, TOWA, YUKI, and YURA)—a perfect 50/50 split.

RYUJI in the Nurse's Office & TOWA in the Staff Room Get Caught

With almost everyone outside caught, the taggers moved indoors to search the building.
In the 1st floor nurse's office, RYUJI—who had found a cabinet at the end of Part 1—was shown with the caption "RYUJI in hiding."
Enter tagger ISSA, with the caption: "Light Blue Oni intimidating-ssa."
Hiding in his spot, RYUJI was stifling his laughter toward the camera, earning the adorable caption "Terrified Samoyed puppy."

{{IMG_ryuji}}

Inevitably, RYUJI was found and tagged.
The taggers even went as far as opening the nurse's office refrigerator to check inside, prompting the caption: "Thoroughly checking even the fridge..."

Meanwhile, in the 1st floor staff room, TOWA—who had squeezed himself into a storage cabinet in Part 1—was still "TOWA in hiding."
Taggers walked in and out of the staff room multiple times, sparking back-to-back crisis alerts: "TOWA in trouble!" and "TOWA in trouble again!"
Finally, the cabinet door swung open, and with TOWA's bewildered cry of "Who even looks here?!", the screen declared "TOWA captured."
However, TOWA immediately pushed back: "Wait, hold on," "I didn't get tagged!"—setting off the most chaotic debate of Part 2 (more on that later).

YUKI in the Music Room & RYOGA's PA System Threats

YUKI, who hadn't locked in a spot by the end of Part 1, initially headed back to the 2nd floor classroom where the taggers originally started.
Captions read: "YUKI arrives at the original classroom" and "The 'hiding in plain sight' strategy?!"
He then relocated to the 2nd floor music room, loving the setup: "Isn't this place amazing?" He tucked behind a desk, cheerfully announcing, "I'm hiding here~" as the caption signaled "Hiding complete."

Around the same time, tagger RYOGA grabbed the PA microphone in the 1st floor broadcasting room.
Starting with a casual "Mic check," he announced over the school speakers: "To everyone still hiding, pretty much everyone's been caught," and "Just give up and come on out!" with the caption noting "RYOGA fully embracing the Oni role."
Right after the broadcast, YUKI was found and tagged in the music room, slumping his shoulders in defeat: "Are you serious... so frustrating!"

{{IMG_yuki}}

At this stage, only three runners remained: SHINHAENG, SIYOUNG, and YURA. The screen flashed: "Only 3 left... But where is SIYOUNG?"
Kept under wraps since Part 1, SIYOUNG's location was still a complete mystery at this point.

{{IMG_three}}

SHINHAENG Spotted from the Rooftop!

SHINHAENG was still perched high up near the gymnasium, just like in Part 1.
With a disclaimer noting "Filmed with special permission from the facility" on screen, SHINHAENG wore a smug, confident grin, looking totally untouchable.

Meanwhile, newly turned taggers KOSUKE and RYUJI were scouring the 3rd floor. When KOSUKE noted, "No sign of anyone here... In that case, it has to be the rooftop," they headed upstairs.
The dimly lit 3rd floor hallway had them spooked, with RYUJI admitting, "Feels like someone's totally here," and "Honestly, it's kinda creepy," while KOSUKE added, "This is turning into a horror game." The caption joked: "Making noise to distract from the fear."

Up on the rooftop, RYUJI wondered, "Surely no one's out here?", but as they looked down from above, they spotted SHINHAENG tucked up high near the gym.
The caption popped up: "Spotted from an unexpected angle!", sending SHINHAENG into a frenzy: "Busted and in pure panic!"
They called out to their fellow taggers from the rooftop, and as the taggers converged, "Cornered SHINHAENG" was quickly captured.
A caption praised KOSUKE and RYUJI for the find: "Red Oni & Yellow Oni looking proud of a job well done."

{{IMG_shinhaeng}}

TOWA's "I Wasn't Tagged!" Commotion

As time ran low, YOSHIKI, who was one of the seekers, made an announcement from the broadcasting room: "5 minutes left!" and "Let's do our best, everyone!"
He even called out to those still hiding, saying, "To the kids who are hiding," "Let's fight fair and square by running!" to urge them to come out.

Appearing as if in response to this announcement was TOWA, who was supposed to have been caught in the staff room.
Standing on the second-floor balcony facing the camera, he proudly declared, "As of right now, I have not been tagged," "If you don't get tagged, you don't become a seeker," and "In other words, I am still a runner!"
He continued, "Like he just said in the announcement, I'm playing fair and square," "Please, feel free to tag me," and added, "Everyone really needs to understand the rules properly," specifically calling out DAIKI: "Especially DAIKI-kun—even though he found me, he didn't tag me!"

{{IMG_towa}}

The on-screen captions read "The Lime Green Seeker convinced he hasn't been tagged" and "The Lime Green Seeker who even mistook which seeker found him," showing that the production team treated TOWA as a seeker.
In fact, on the midpoint status board, TOWA was already listed in the "Seeker" column.

During the final reveal back in the classroom, TOWA greeted everyone with, "Hello, I wasn't caught," with the caption reading "TOWA, who still thinks he hasn't been tagged."
That was when ISSA confessed, "I tagged you, though?" and "I tagged you silently."
When they replayed the staff room scene, a hand reached out quietly from the side as TOWA emerged from behind a cabinet, accompanied by on-screen text reading "*He WAS tagged."
The punchline was that it was actually ISSA, not DAIKI, who had caught TOWA, and TOWA hadn't realized it until the very end!

YURA and SIYOUNG Survived! Where Were Their Hiding Spots?

The final two remaining were YURA and SIYOUNG.
In the gymnasium, KOSUKE, one of the seekers, tilted his head in confusion, saying, "I feel like there's definitely one spot we haven't looked at," but time ran out. With DAIKI's shout of "It's over~!", the hide-and-seek game officially ended.

YURA: Inside the Trees Behind the Gymnasium

YURA didn't move from behind the trees behind the gymnasium—the spot he chose in the first part—until the very end.
As the sun began to set, he prayed, "I hope it gets darker," and "Please let 20 minutes pass like this," while the caption read "YURA continues to stay hidden without being found by anyone."
He blended into the branches and leaves so thoroughly that he even whispered to himself, "They're definitely not finding this spot," and "Can you even see my face?"

{{IMG_yura}}

The seekers did suspect the outside area—ISSA commented, "Isn't bushes like this the only option left?", and RYUJI mentioned, "(YURA) was in the track team, so he'd probably rely on his speed."
During the post-game recap, when YUKI mentioned, "Apparently he was in the bushes," and YOSHIKI added, "Behind the trees," the members were shocked, exclaiming, "We checked behind the gymnasium so many times!"
In Part 1, YURA had said, "I don't have much of a presence, so I hope I don't get found," and he successfully survived just as he said!

SIYOUNG: 1st Floor, Room Opposite the Broadcasting Room

SIYOUNG's hiding spot, which had been hidden as "1st Floor ???" since Part 1, was finally revealed during the recap.
When SIYOUNG explained, "1st floor," "opposite the broadcasting room," the members nodded in understanding, "Ah, that place!"
That means he was hiding directly opposite the broadcasting room, where RYOGA and YOSHIKI had been making announcements over and over!

{{IMG_siyoung}}

However, when ISSA chimed in with, "Isn't that area off-limits?", the caption immediately changed to "SIYOUNG Suspected of Rule Violation."
When everyone went to check together, they found a "DO NOT ENTER" sign posted on the door of the room labeled "Studio," prompting bursts of laughter from the members: "Off-limits?!" and "You were in here?!"
SIYOUNG defended himself by replying, "I can't read kanji," which convinced everyone with a "Fair point!"
Even though they had established the rule in Part 1 that "entering off-limits areas is strictly forbidden," no official ruling was made, and the whole situation wrapped up in laughter.

{{IMG_studio}}

By the way, when they returned to the classroom, YURA threw a quick remark at SIYOUNG: "I didn't go into any off-limits areas."
He proudly pointed out that he had survived within the rules, making for a hilarious contrast between the two survivors' methods.

Recap and Ending

During the recap back in the classroom, DAIKI reflected, "It's tough to make calls on things like who's actually a seeker."
ISSA revealed that after becoming a seeker, he intentionally "acted like I was still running away," prompting comments from members like "That's such Mafia player behavior!"
Because infection tag doesn't visually distinguish seekers from runners, this episode was packed with chaotic fun where you couldn't trust your teammates—including TOWA's tagging commotion.

At the end, everyone was completely exhausted, saying "I'm tired." DAIKI said, "Shall we head home? I'll walk home today."
KEITO fired back, "I'm a speed demon, so I'm running home!" and the episode wrapped up with the members sharing feelings like "So frustrating!" and "That was so fun!"

Where Is the School Shooting Location?

Part 2 appears to be filmed at the same school as Part 1 and EP.3.
The shooting location for EP.3 was identified as the school studio "GYODA+ (Gyoda Plus)" (formerly Gyoda City Ota Higashi Elementary School) in Gyoda City, Saitama Prefecture. Part 2 also features the signature zigzag-roofed gymnasium and the schoolyard with red and blue jungle gyms.
Newly featured areas in Part 2 like the broadcasting room, music room, and rooftop share the same building structure and balcony handrails as seen in Part 1.

Highlights of EP.4 Part 2

The biggest highlight is how the tables turn as the number of seekers grows.
ISSA, who was caught first, became the "Light Blue Seeker" and showed more enthusiasm than anyone else, contributing to almost every capture in Part 2—from pincer-attacking KOSUKE and trapping RYUJI in the nurse's office, to silently tagging TOWA.
His active performance truly fit the caption: "Hard to believe he was a runner just moments ago!"

Another highlight is TOWA, who confidently made a "runner declaration" without realizing he had already been caught.
The contrast between him explaining the rules on the balcony while referencing the announcement, and the replay featuring the caption "*He WAS tagged," made for one of the most memorable scenes in the series.
Additionally, the hiding styles of the two survivors were completely contrasting—YURA remaining motionless blending into the trees, versus SIYOUNG hiding in a potentially off-limits room—making for an ending filled with personality right to the finish.
