KO1KEYZ are now in their sixth week since debut.
Looking through Japanese TV listings and the official site for November 8-14, the biggest news this week is that YOSHIKI appears on Nippon TV's talk show "Odoru! Sanma Goten!!" on Tuesday, Nov 10 at 20:00 as a representative of Nara Prefecture.
The week opens with day two of the "2026 KO1KEYZ 1ST FAN MEETING IN SEOUL" on Sunday, Nov 8, and on Thursday, Nov 12, DAIKI and RYUJI join Nippon TV's live lunchtime show "Hirunandesu!" as studio guests.
KNTV also re-airs the subtitled Show! Music Core episode featuring KO1KEYZ that same Thursday morning.

This post lists the TV appearances along with MV airplay, the YouTube update day and the LAPOSTA 2027 fan club ticket deadline in date order.
*Information as of November 6, 2026 (all times JST). Listings for Nov 14 have not been released yet. Broadcast details may change, so please check official sites and TV listings for the latest information.

YOSHIKI on "Sanma Goten" as Team Nara (Tue 11/10)

YOSHIKI joins Nippon TV's prime-time talk show "Odoru! Sanma Goten!!" on Tuesday, November 10 from 20:00.
The theme is a "neighboring prefecture rivalry battle" in which guests from Kyoto, Nara, Shiga and Mie promote the charms of their hometowns.
According to the listing, YOSHIKI is on Team Nara alongside Soichi Nakaoka (Rotchi), judo legend Tadahiro Nomura, YUINEKO (GALEDGE.) and Yuriyan Retriever.
It is one of the first solo appearances by a KO1KEYZ member on a prime-time terrestrial talk show, so it is well worth recording.

DAIKI and RYUJI on "Hirunandesu!" (Thu 11/12)

DAIKI and RYUJI appear as studio guests on Nippon TV's live lunchtime variety show "Hirunandesu!" on Thursday, November 12 from 11:55 to 13:55.
The listing names them under studio guests alongside the comedy duo Nasunakanishi.
That day's main segment is a budget trip to Lake Kawaguchi in Yamanashi, with a total of 25,000 yen per person including a night's stay and two meals.
It is a rare chance to see the leader DAIKI and the group's mood-maker RYUJI on a weekday live show in Japan.

Seoul fan meeting, Day 2 (Sun 11/8)

Day two of the "2026 KO1KEYZ 1ST FAN MEETING IN SEOUL" takes place on November 8 at BLUESQUARE WOORI WON BANKING HALL.
The fan club booth, the KO1KEYZ Chat "Plus Chat BOOTH" and the merch and album sales at BLUE SQUARE NEMO run from 10:00-13:30 and 14:30-18:00 local time.
Every 75,000 won spent on official merch gets you a random solo trading card while supplies last.

KNTV re-airs Show! Music Core #966 (Thu 11/12)

On Thursday, November 12 at 9:45, the Japanese CS channel KNTV re-airs the subtitled version of MBC's Show! Music Core #966.
KO1KEYZ are listed among the performers along with BOYNEXTDOOR, CRAVITY, NCT WISH, Hearts2Hearts and KickFlip.
This episode first aired in Japan on November 3, so the rerun is a second chance to catch it.

MV airplay and YouTube

KO1KEYZ are on the planned playlist for M-ON!'s "M-ON! Request Hour" on Sunday, November 8 at 22:00.
The official YouTube series "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ!" updates every Thursday at 21:00, so the next episode is due on November 12.
A "Koi GAME" game effect was also released on TikTok on November 5.

LAPOSTA 2027 fan club presale ends 11/15

The KO1KEYZ OFFICIAL FANCLUB presale for LAPOSTA 2027 (Tokyo Dome in January and Kyocera Dome Osaka in February 2027) is open from November 4 at 12:00 until November 15 at 23:59 JST.
Results will be announced on November 26 at 20:00, and reserved seats cost 15,400 yen including tax.

What's next in November?

After Seoul, KO1KEYZ will perform at the "2026 MAMA AWARDS" at Kyocera Dome Osaka on Saturday, November 21, and at "SAPPORO COLLECTION 2026 AUTUMN/WINTER" in Sapporo on Sunday, November 22.
We will add any new TV appearances as they are announced.
