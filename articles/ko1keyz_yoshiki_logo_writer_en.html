Whenever KO1KEYZ sign a wall or board on TV or at events, there is always a chubby hand-drawn "KO1KEYZ" logo sitting in the middle of the twelve autographs.
The person drawing it turned out to be YOSHIKI (Yoshiki Yada), the member whose color is pink.
On September 26, YOSHIKI revealed it himself in a message to fans: "Oh yeah, I'm the one who draws the KO1KEYZ logo every time!"
This article collects every version of the logo so far, from KCON LA and CDTV to Yoru no Brunch and GirlsAward, with photos of how the lettering changes from stage to stage.

YOSHIKI draws the KO1KEYZ logo: what exactly did he say?

When:Around 3:08 p.m. on Saturday, September 26, 2026 (Japan time)

Where:KO1KEYZ Chat, the group's fan messaging service

Message:"Oh yeah, I'm the one who draws the KO1KEYZ logo every time!"

YOSHIKI revealed that he is the logo artist on the afternoon of September 26.
As if it had just crossed his mind, he sent "Oh yeah, I'm the one who draws the KO1KEYZ logo every time!" on KO1KEYZ Chat, then added, "Looking forward to today."
That was the day of "Rakuten GirlsAward 2026 AUTUMN/WINTER" at Makuhari Messe, KO1KEYZ's first appearance at the event.
"Today" most likely meant the GirlsAward stage.

The words "every time" suggest this was not a one-off: drawing the group logo seems to be YOSHIKI's job.
In fact, the official footage from CDTV Live! Live! on September 14 shows YOSHIKI signing first and writing "KO1KEYZ" on the wall, so many viewers had probably already guessed it was him.
His message confirmed that guess.
Because KO1KEYZ Chat is a paid service, we are sharing only the wording here, not a screenshot of the message.

YOSHIKI was born on June 18, 2004, comes from Nara Prefecture, and won his debut spot by finishing 2nd on PRODUCE 101 JAPAN SHINSEKAI.
His singing, which took him into the top 10 of the TV singing contest "Gen'eki Kaō JAPAN," usually gets the attention, but the logo story puts a spotlight on his eye for art and design too.

KCON LA 2026: pink balloon letters

Dates:August 14–16, 2026, KCON LA 2026 (Los Angeles, USA)

Logo:Balloon letters in pink pen on a black wall

Message:"KCON LA ドキドキキュンキュン♡" (roughly "KCON LA, heart pounding♡")

The earliest logo we could confirm is the one on the signing wall at KCON LA 2026 in August.
KO1KEYZ took the stage on August 14 local time and gave the world premiere of the Korean version of their debut song "KO1KEYZ."
You can read more about that day in our guide to watching KO1KEYZ at KCON LA 2026.

On the wall, a big "KO1KEYZ" in pink pen sits in the center, with all twelve autographs circling it.
YOSHIKI's own signature is at the upper left of the logo, and below it the members added the message "KCON LA ドキドキキュンキュン♡."
The letters are drawn as outlines and left unfilled, the same approach he used later at CDTV.

At the time, nobody had said who drew the logo.
Given his "every time," though, it is safe to assume the KCON LA logo is YOSHIKI's work as well.
That means he has been drawing the group's name since their pre-debut overseas stages.

At CDTV, he spent about 40 seconds carefully drawing the logo

Date:September 14, 2026, KO1KEYZ's first appearance on "CDTV Live! Live!"

Official video:The sign movie on CDTV's official Instagram (from the start to about 0:44)

Logo:White balloon letters on a black wall, with YOSHIKI's signature just below

The clearest look at YOSHIKI drawing the logo comes from KO1KEYZ's first appearance on CDTV Live! Live!
The show's traditional black signing wall was released as a "sign movie" on CDTV's official Instagram.
The video opens with pink-haired YOSHIKI facing the wall: of the twelve, he was the first to pick up the pen.

He starts with "K," "O," and "1," tracing each letter's outline one by one and slowly working to the right.
In the video, it takes him about 40 seconds to finish the seven characters of "KO1KEYZ."
The other members each sign in around ten seconds, so he clearly took his time.

Once the logo was done, he added his own signature just to the lower right, waved at the camera with both hands, and handed over to the next member.
Setting the logo's size and position first makes it easier for the other eleven to arrange their signatures around it.
Having the logo artist go first makes sense for the layout of the whole wall.

On the finished wall, the twelve signatures surround the logo.
The logo is drawn with a single white pen as an outline, with the inside left empty.
YURA's katakana signature sits to the right of the logo, and you can compare each member's signature in our article on the fan meeting sign balls.

The Yoru no Brunch sign card: even the show title is hand-lettered

Broadcast:From 0:26 a.m. on Wednesday, September 23, 2026 (late night), TBS "Yoru no Brunch"

Released:The show's official X shared the sign card on September 22

Design:The show title in balloon letters, with cat ears and whiskers around the photo

Late on September 23, KO1KEYZ made their first appearance on TBS's "Yoru no Brunch" and took on a fill-in-the-lyrics quiz using their debut song.
The day before the broadcast, the show's official X shared the group's sign card, noting that "they drew Appurin for us too."

On the black paper, the show title "よるのブランチ" is written in puffy balloon letters.
Each character is filled in a different color (white, light blue, pink, yellow, and green), with a moon and stars scattered around.
All twelve members signed in their member colors, and YOSHIKI's signature is the pink one in the bottom row.

The group selfie in the middle has cat ears, whiskers, and a tail drawn around it in white pen, turning the whole photo into a cat face.
KO1KEYZ have a song called "Neko" (Cat), and some fans may also have thought of the family cat YOSHIKI has talked about.
We do not know whose idea the cat was, but it is a playful card that feels very KO1KEYZ.

One caveat: YOSHIKI said he draws the KO1KEYZ logo, and he did not say who lettered the show title on this card.
Still, the thickness and roundness of the letters, and the way the outlines are drawn first and then filled, look a lot like his KO1KEYZ logo.
It seems quite possible that he lettered the show title too.

At GirlsAward, a pixel-style logo, and a pixel Appurin too

Date:September 26, 2026, "Rakuten GirlsAward 2026 AUTUMN/WINTER" (Makuhari Messe)

Logo:Blocky, pixel-style 3D letters

Highlight:TOWA's character "Appurin" also appears in pixel art

On the day YOSHIKI said "I'm the one who draws the logo," a completely new version appeared.
The sign board at the venue shows pixel-style 3D letters that look like a video game screen.
The outlines are drawn as stepped, blocky lines, with diagonal hatching at the lower right as a shadow so the letters seem to pop off the board.

The board was the GirlsAward design, pink gingham with a Monchhichi illustration.
On stage that day, KO1KEYZ performed three songs: "KO1KEYZ," "Neko," and "Koi GAME."
The game-screen pixel letters fit nicely with "Koi GAME," a song that compares love to a game.
YOSHIKI has not explained the choice, but thinking of the logo as something he adapts to each occasion makes it even more fun to look at.

The other highlight is "Appurin," next to TOWA's signature.
Appurin is an apple character TOWA has been drawing since elementary school, and it is normally a round apple.
On this board, however, the apple's outline, stem, arms, and legs are all blocky pixel art, perfectly matching the logo.

We do not know yet whether the pixel Appurin was drawn by its creator TOWA or by YOSHIKI to match his logo.
Either way, the board shows the members having fun playing along with the logo design.
You can read the story behind the character in our article on TOWA's apple character Appurin.

Not the official symbol mark: what makes the hand-drawn logo different

Official symbol mark:A graphic built from the kanji 恋 ("love"), announced August 5, 2026

Hand-drawn logo:The "KO1KEYZ" lettering YOSHIKI draws at each event

KO1KEYZ also have an official symbol mark, announced on August 5.
It breaks the kanji 恋 down into square pieces and is used on merchandise and official announcements.
As explained in our article on the meaning of the symbol mark, it comes from a completely different design idea.

The "KO1KEYZ" written during signings, on the other hand, is YOSHIKI's own handwriting.
Putting the versions we found side by side, they share a few traits.

What the hand-drawn logos have in common

  • The "1" in the group name is always written as a number
  • At KCON LA and CDTV, balloon letters drawn as unfilled outlines
  • At GirlsAward, stepped outlines with a hatched shadow for a 3D look
  • The logo goes in the center, with the other signatures around it

Because the same person draws them, the spacing and balance feel familiar even when the style changes.
Next time you see a KO1KEYZ signing wall, take a look at what the logo in the middle looks like.

More signs of YOSHIKI's artistic eye

ViVi coloring challenge:Colored everything pink and added blush that was not in the original

Detail:Filled the hair with a fine grid pattern and scattered hearts around it

The logo is not the only place his sense for art and design shows.
In ViVi's coloring challenge with the magazine's "Angels" characters, released on ViVi's official YouTube, he finished a drawing with pink hair, pink clothes, and pink boots.
He explained, "Coloring it in matters, but I also added little things that weren't there originally, like blush on the cheeks, to make her cute."

The hair was filled with a fine grid pattern, and small hearts were scattered all around.
Even with only five minutes, he did not stop at coloring and thought about what he could add to make it cuter, which feels very much like YOSHIKI.
Changing the logo style for each occasion may come from the same instinct.
