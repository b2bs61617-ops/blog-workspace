# Who Sings What in KO1KEYZ's "Run Again"? All 12 Members' Parts!

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the encore of "1ST FAN MEETING," the fan meeting that kicked off in August 2026, all 12 members of KO1KEYZ rode a trolley through the venue to perform "Run Again -KO1KEYZ ver.-".<br>
The song was originally a special-stage number performed by every trainee at the final of "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD," <strong>written and composed by JO1's Ren Kawashiri (川尻蓮)</strong>.<br>
The highlight of the "KO1KEYZ ver." performed at the fan meeting is how the 12 members split up the lines. In this article, we've mapped out the part distribution based on fans' listening notes from the performance, swapping the member emoji used in those posts for each member's romanized stage name.<br>
Note that the official audio and lyrics haven't been released yet, so the parts and lyrics below should be read as a tentative version based on fan transcription.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What is "Run Again"? Performed at the 1ST FAN MEETING encore</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<div style="border:1px solid #ddd9d3;border-left:4px solid #8a8378;border-radius:4px;padding:10px 16px;margin:0 0 16px 0;background:#f7f6f4;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Title:</strong>Run Again -KO1KEYZ ver.-</p>
<p style="margin:4px 0 0 0;"><strong>Original:</strong>A special-stage song from the final of "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD" (performed by every trainee)</p>
<p style="margin:4px 0 0 0;"><strong>Written &amp; composed by:</strong>Ren Kawashiri (JO1)</p>
<p style="margin:4px 0 0 0;"><strong>First KO1KEYZ ver. performance:</strong>The encore of "2026 KO1KEYZ 1ST FAN MEETING" (Tokyo/Hyogo), which opened on August 21, 2026</p>
<p style="margin:4px 0 0 0;"><strong>Staging:</strong>Members rode a trolley around the venue, tossing autographed balls to the crowd while singing. Performed back-to-back with "Shinsekai -KO1KEYZ ver.-"</p>
</div>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>"Run Again" was performed as a special stage by every trainee at the June 2026 final of "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN THE NEW WORLD."<br>
It was written and composed by JO1's Ren Kawashiri, who was present at the final venue himself and watched the song he'd written performed on stage.<br>
For KO1KEYZ &#8211; a group born from that audition show &#8211; to sing this song again as a "12-member ver." at their very first fan meeting before debut gave the setlist special meaning for fans who followed their time on the show.<br>
At the encore, it was performed with members riding a trolley around the venue and tossing autographed balls, running straight into "Shinsekai -KO1KEYZ ver.-".<br>
You can read more about the full flow of the fan meeting's first day in <a href="https://chomoand-1.com/en/ko1keyz1st-fan-meeting-tokyo-f-en-12764" target="_blank" rel="noopener">our rundown of KO1KEYZ's 1st Fan Meeting Day 1 setlist, seating chart and trolley staging</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Notable points in the part distribution</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A few things stand out when you look at how the 12 members split this song.<br>
First, <strong>every one of the 12 members gets a solo line</strong> &#8211; a big feature of this song. Rather than leaning on a handful of members, it hands off like a relay from the first verse all the way to the outro, reflecting the fact that the original was a special stage for every trainee.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The English hook <strong>"Cause you'll be nothing / if you forget truly who you are"</strong> changes hands every time it appears.<br>
ISSA sings it at the first pre-chorus and again right before the final chorus, RYUJI takes it at the second pre-chorus, and SHINHAENG sings it in the outro &#8211; meaning ISSA covers this key line twice out of its four appearances.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rapped-out <strong>"Ay run, there's plenty of runway / run, I'll carry my scars too" is TOWA's</strong>, followed by <strong>"This one step opens up the future / a new world we build, you and me," sung together by DAIKI and TOWA</strong>.<br>
<strong>KOSUKE</strong> then catches it with <strong>"Just follow me"</strong> right into the lead-in to the final chorus &#8211; the song's big turning point.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The chorus line <strong>"together" ("僕らで") is sung by KEITO in both Chorus 1 and the Final Chorus</strong> &#8211; a short but consistently repeated part.<br>
The song's opening line (the very first line of Verse 1) belongs to YUKI, while the closing line, <strong>"I'll be right here" ("そばにいるよ"), goes to YURA</strong> &#8211; a nice touch of different members bookending the track.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What "Run Again"'s lyrics mean</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>True to its title, "Run Again" is built around a single message: <strong>"no matter how many times you fall, get up and run again."</strong><br>
Lines like "if you fall again, hug your knees," "even a trail full of mistakes," and "even the long way around becomes meaningful" keep this forward-looking theme going, mistakes and all.<br>
Sung by 12 members who came through a win-or-lose audition show, the lyrics end up mirroring KO1KEYZ's own road to debut.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As the song goes on, the focus shifts from "myself" to "you."<br>
Lines like "don't let go of the hand you grabbed while you were lost," "that's exactly why I believe," and finally "I'll be right here" can also be read as words for the person running alongside them &#8211; their fans.<br>
That reading lines up neatly with the trolley staging, riding through the crowd and tossing autographed balls during the encore.<br>
Since Ren Kawashiri wrote this song specifically for the "New World" show, the line "a new world we build, you and me" carries extra weight in the KO1KEYZ ver.</p>
