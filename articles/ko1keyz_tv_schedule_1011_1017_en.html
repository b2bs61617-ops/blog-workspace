We checked a full week of Japanese TV listings and the official website for their second week, October 11 (Sun) to October 17 (Sat), and two appearances have been officially announced: SBS "Inkigayo" in Korea on Sun 10/11, and TV Asahi's "M:ZINE" late on Fri 10/16.
M:ZINE took a break from KO1KEYZ last week, and this second episode features a special variety challenge called "Variety 101 Shinsekai."

Below you'll find the dates and highlights of both appearances, plus music channels airing the MV, M COUNTDOWN's subtitled reruns, and the week's YouTube and Lemino schedule, all in date order.

What Happens in M:ZINE Week 2?

Air time: Sat, October 17, 2026, 1:30-1:50 (listed in Japan as Fri 10/16 at 25:30)

Channel: TV Asahi

Segment: Variety 101 Shinsekai

The highlight of the week is TV Asahi's music variety show "M:ZINE."
KO1KEYZ are the show's featured artist for October, and following the first episode in the early hours of 10/3, the second airs in the early hours of 10/17.
The official KO1KEYZ website lists it as October 16 (Fri) 25:30-25:50.

According to the TV listing, this episode is a special called "Variety 101 Shinsekai," a search for the group's true variety "star."
The title is a play on "PRODUCE 101 JAPAN SHINSEKAI," a fitting idea for a group born from an audition show.
One round is a push-up endurance battle, with all twelve members going all out.
The final round is a "heart-fluttering kabedon battle," featuring a character named "Koshin" played by MC Hiroto Wakai of Mrs. GREEN APPLE.

The episode ends by crowning a "Variety King" and a "Ponkotsu (clumsy) Star," so the big question is who takes which title.
Along with KO1KEYZ, the cast includes Hiroto Wakai, Kan Yamazoe of Aiseki Start, and TV Asahi announcer Mio Hayashi.
It's a 20-minute late-night show broadcast in the Kanto region, so set a recording if you're in Japan.

SBS Inkigayo on Sunday 10/11: Can You Watch It in Japan?

Air time: Sun, October 11, 2026, 15:15-16:50

Channel: SBS (Korea)

Japanese TV: No simulcast listed

After M COUNTDOWN (10/8), Music Bank (10/9), and Show! Music Core (10/10) during debut week, KO1KEYZ complete the round of Korea's four major music shows with SBS "Inkigayo" on Sunday, October 11.
The official website lists the broadcast as 15:15-16:50.

However, we couldn't find any Japanese cable or satellite channel simulcasting this Inkigayo episode.
Inkigayo stages are usually uploaded to the show's official YouTube channel after broadcast, so that's the most practical way to watch from Japan.

KO1KEYZ also have a fansign and 1:1 video call event in Korea on Sunday, October 18, so their Korean promotions continue next week.
Appearances on M COUNTDOWN (10/15) or Music Bank (10/16) in week two had not been announced as of October 11; we'll update this post if they are.

The Day-After-Debut M COUNTDOWN With Japanese Subtitles

Mnet Japan airs each Thursday's live M COUNTDOWN again with Japanese subtitles on Sunday at 18:00, Tuesday at 10:00, and Wednesday at midnight.
This week's subtitled slots are Sun 10/11 18:00-20:00, Tue 10/13 10:00-12:00, and Thu 10/15 0:00-2:00.

The "KO1KEYZ" MV on MUSIC ON! TV and Space Shower TV

Japanese music channels are also scheduled to play the debut MV.
KO1KEYZ are listed among the artists for MUSIC ON! TV's "Request Hour" on Sun 10/11 from 20:00 to 21:00, a show built from viewer requests.
On Mon 10/12, "KO1KEYZ" is on the playlist for both Space Shower TV "Now Hits!" slots, 7:00-8:00 and 11:00-12:00, as the second song each time.

Unica Vision Special in Shinjuku Through 10/13
