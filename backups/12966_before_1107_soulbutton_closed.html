The shops and spots they visited

Here is a quick overview of where the three stopped in Hongdae.
Address captions appeared for three places — YoYogurt, Soul Button and Nemamdaero Phonecase — while the keyring shop and the self photo studio had no caption (estimated from the footage).

(1) "YoYogurt Hongdae Main Gate branch", where they ate yangji-gamno

What they ate: Yangji-gamno = a dessert of mango + tapioca + sago
Shop: YoYogurt Hongdae Main Gate branch (유요거트 홍대정문점)
Address: Bldg 101, B-147, 10 Hongik-ro, Mapo-gu, Seoul (right by Hongik Univ. Station Exit 9)

The first shop is YoYogurt Hongdae Main Gate branch, which appears in the video alongside the exchange "Is this yangji-gamno?"
Yangji-gamno (yeung-ji-gam-lo) is a Hong Kong-style dessert, introduced in the video as "a new Chinese dessert". This shop's signature is a "solid yangji-gamno": half a mango topped with Greek yogurt, with grapefruit pulp and sago pearls.
The three took theirs in cups and ate as they walked. "Simple but tasty," "the mango is good," "this one has pearls," they said approvingly.

The "butter tteok" and bear straw from Hongdae street stalls

What they ate: Dubai-chocolate-style cookie / butter tteok (butter tok)
What they bought: Bear butter-tteok straw (straw cover; bought by DAIKI)
Shop: Hongdae street stalls (no name caption)

Partway through the dessert crawl, a "Dubai-chocolate-style cookie" that you can buy at Korean convenience stores also appears.
Everyone in the dorm apparently keeps a stock, and they shared that "YOSHIKI eats five at a time" and "he keeps them in the fridge".
Next, the three went hunting for butter tteok (butter tok), a trending sweet "coming up right after the Dubai cookie". The one they bought from a stall was hard and crunchy outside, chewy inside, with a toasty flavor. DAIKI dips his in condensed milk; SHINHAENG doesn't.
They liked it enough to say "if we find it again, let's buy ten".

DAIKI also bought a "bear butter-tteok straw" (straw cover) from a stall.
When they put it in a drink, all three got excited, calling it "chan-kawa" (beyond super-cute).

(2) The cafe "Soul Button", where they got drinks

What they ordered: Matcha cream / matcha caramel / rose milk tea
Shop: Soul Button (소울버튼)
Address: 1F, 21 Wausan-ro 18-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul (open 12:00–22:00, closed Mondays)

For a drink break they stopped at a cafe shown in the address caption as Soul Button (소울버튼).
Known for its blue exterior, kiosk ordering and cat-shaped souffle cake, the shop prompts a "Do you like cats?" bit in the video.
The three ordered matcha cream, matcha caramel and rose milk tea together, with DAIKI asking, "What's rose milk tea?"

(3) "Nemamdaero Phonecase Hongdae", where they made bead dolls

What they made: Alphabet-bead "KO1LY" strap / charms / pipe-cleaner doll
Shop: Nemamdaero Phonecase Hongdae (내맘대로 폰케이스)
Address: 3F, Big Mama Bldg, 21 Wausan-ro 19-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul (open 12:00–21:00)

The "secret next place" turned out to be the DIY workshop shop Nemamdaero Phonecase Hongdae.
It offers "unusual handmade experiences" like phone decoration, bead straps, pipe-cleaner dolls, decorated cases and slime.
The three made a "KO1LY" strap with alphabet beads, and also charms and pipe-cleaner dolls. SIYOUNG's strap combined "a dolphin I've loved since I was a baby (a white figure)" with "a cloud bead (= something I want to carry every day = KO1LY)".
DAIKI went all-green throughout, saying "I just react to anything green".

Where is the self photo studio "Shinmera"?

What they did: Took photos at a self photo studio (nicknamed "SHINHAENG camera = Shinmera")
Location: Near Hongdae's Rainbow Road ("Street You Want to Walk") — no name caption

After the doll-making, the three took photos at a self photo studio they dubbed "SHINHAENG camera (Shinmera)".
They did a heart pose, then SIYOUNG's "roar" pose, and finally a "surprised face" pose.
This studio had no address caption and its name is unknown, but the footage just before they enter shows a 7-Eleven, a coin karaoke, a vape shop, a cylindrical glass building and a rainbow-painted crosswalk, which places it near the "Street You Want to Walk" (Rainbow Road) in front of Hongik University Station.
Inside, the studio had a red heart-patterned wall and a concrete wall with framed, faded jeans.

(4) Where did they buy the matching keyrings for all 12 members?

What they bought: Matching animal/character keyrings for all 12 members (about ₩6,800–16,500 = about 750–1,800 yen each)
Brand: ARTBOX (아트박스) — identified as ARTBOX's own brands, e.g. the GEWEGI alien and the "I Love You" rabbit
Store: Appears to be the ARTBOX on 1F of AK& Hongdae (AK Plaza) — no name caption

Last, they went to a keyring shop to buy "couple items" (matching goods) for all 12 members.
DAIKI (the "green man" who reacts to anything green) picked out animal and character keyrings, and back at the dorm handed them out one by one with a "Guess who?" game.
There is no name caption, but the keyrings are almost certainly ARTBOX (아트박스) products.
TOWA's green alien is ARTBOX's "GEWEGI Alien Plush Keyring" (with headphones), and KEITO's rabbit is ARTBOX's "I Love You Aurora Rabbit". After release, an ARTBOX seller on Qoo10 even used the video's polaroids to put up per-member product pages labeled "KO1KEYZ ___'s pick".
Given the wall of "PLUSH KEYRING" and "MAGNET PAIR KEYCHAIN" stock (₩6,800–16,500) and the fact that they pass through a station-connected corridor (a tourist map, an in-building "Vintage & Select Market" sign) just before entering, the store appears to be the ARTBOX on 1F of AK& Hongdae (AK Plaza).

In the handout scene back at the dorm, each member holds their own keyring for a polaroid-style shot.

KEITO says he "chose the aurora rabbit for its color, and the long ears were an afterthought".
YUKI's brown monkey is the same one he normally keeps on his bag, so SIYOUNG's pick matched his personal item.
RYUJI's white fluffy one prompted an on-the-spot "polar bear?" "no, Samoyed" exchange.

You can get these keyrings in Japan too.
The easiest option is Qoo10, where an ARTBOX seller has set up per-member product pages (the "I Love You" Aurora Rabbit is about 1,980 yen, with further discounts during the Mega-wari sale).
The alien (with headphones) is usually around 1,850 yen, or about 1,480 yen during Mega-wari.
For a physical store, there is ARTBOX in Shin-Okubo, Tokyo, and for Korea's official online store, POOM also stocks them.
The local prices seen on the video's display racks were ₩6,800–16,500 (the alien ₩8,800), so buying in Korea is cheapest.
