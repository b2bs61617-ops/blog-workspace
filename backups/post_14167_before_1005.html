KO1KEYZ, the 12-member group formed through PRODUCE 101 JAPAN SHINSEKAI, makes its simultaneous Japan-Korea debut on Wednesday, October 7.
We went through a full week of Japanese TV listings for debut week (Sunday, October 4 to Saturday, October 10), and the officially announced TV appearances are Nippon TV's "DayDay." on Wed 10/7 and Mnet's "M COUNTDOWN," broadcast live in Japan and Korea, on Thu 10/8.

Below you'll find each show's date, channel and what to look out for, plus the streaming content and MV airings from the same week, and even a show that is taking a week off.
*Information as of October 3, 2026. All times are Japan Standard Time (same as Korea). Broadcast details may change, so please check the official channels for the latest information.

What time is Nippon TV's "DayDay." on 10/7?

Airtime:Wednesday, October 7, 2026, 9:00-11:10

Channel:Nippon TV network (Japanese terrestrial TV)

On the morning of their debut, KO1KEYZ will appear on Nippon TV's daytime information show "DayDay."
The appearance is listed in the media section of the KO1KEYZ official website for October 7, 9:00-11:10.
The show is hosted by Shinichi Takeda and Ryota Yamasato (Nankai Candies) and covers news, entertainment and trending topics.

KO1KEYZ already appeared live on "DayDay." on June 8, before their debut, taking on a "101-second self-PR relay" where each member pitched their own strengths.
This time they return about four months later as a debuted group, so it will be fun to see how they liven up the studio.
The segment name, time slot and whether it is live had not been announced as of October 3, so it's safest to tune in from the 9:00 start.

Mnet "M COUNTDOWN" on 10/8: how to watch

Airtime:Thursday, October 8, 2026, 18:00-20:00

Channel:Mnet (simulcast live in Japan and Korea)

Where to watch:Mnet (Korea), Mnet Japan (CS), Mnet Plus

The day after their debut, KO1KEYZ will appear on "M COUNTDOWN," as announced on the official website.
M COUNTDOWN is one of Korea's flagship weekly music shows, and Mnet Japan carries it live at the same time as Korea, so fans in both countries can watch the stage together.
Japanese listings also show the 10/8 18:00-20:00 slot as "[LIVE] M COUNTDOWN."

Because KO1KEYZ debuts in Japan and Korea at the same time, this stage also marks the start of their Korean promotions.
At KCON LA 2026's "M COUNTDOWN STAGE," they premiered the Korean version of their debut song "KO1KEYZ," so many fans are curious which song and which version they will perform this time.
The setlist had not been announced as of October 3.
If you can't watch live on TV, Mnet Plus, which the official site also lists, is another option.

Streaming content and MV airings the same week

MV airing:Space Shower TV, early hours of Tue 10/6

Debut Day Special Livestream (10/7, around 14:00)

It isn't TV, but on debut day KO1KEYZ will hold a "Debut Day Special Livestream" on their official YouTube channel.
It is scheduled to start around 14:00, and the group is collecting ideas for what to do on the stream through the hashtag "#お願いKO1KEYZ" on social media.
Since it's on YouTube, international fans can watch for free, so send in your request before the day if there's something you want to see.

"KO1KEYZ Gakuen #2" and "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ!" on 10/8

From 19:00 on Thursday, October 8, episode #2 of Lemino's exclusive variety show "KO1KEYZ Gakuen" will be released.
Like #1, it is for Lemino Premium (paid) subscribers, and follows the 12 members as "class presidents" taking on special lessons.
At 21:00, a new episode of "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ!," which updates every Thursday on the official YouTube channel, is free to watch.
For the difference between these two similarly named shows, see our article comparing "KO1! KO1! KO1KEYZ" and "KO1KEYZ Gakuen".

October 8 is also DAIKI's 22nd birthday.
With M COUNTDOWN, Gakuen #2 and the YouTube episode all on the same day, there may be a birthday moment somewhere along the way.

The "KO1KEYZ" MV on Space Shower TV

While the members themselves don't appear, the playlist for Space Shower TV's "Weekly Ranking on YouTube" (Tuesday 10/6, 1:00-4:00) includes KO1KEYZ's "KO1KEYZ."
The ranking is based on YouTube views, which shows how much the MV has been played even before the official debut.
The channel notes that the songs aired may change without notice.

Which show is off this week? Next "M:ZINE" airdate

M:ZINE on Sat 10/10, 1:30:no KO1KEYZ segment (Korea reporting special)

Next KO1KEYZ episodes:late nights of Fri 10/16 and Fri 10/23, 25:30-25:50

TV Asahi's music variety show "M:ZINE" picked KO1KEYZ as its featured artist for October, and the first episode aired late on Friday 10/2.
However, the listing for the late-night Friday 10/9 episode (10/10 at 1:30) shows a "Korea whirlwind reporting tour special," and KO1KEYZ is not among the cast.
The KO1KEYZ official website also lists the M:ZINE airdates as 10/2, 10/16 and 10/23.
The second and third episodes air late on Friday 10/16 and Friday 10/23 at 25:30-25:50 (Kanto region), and an extended 90-minute version is scheduled on CS TV Asahi Channel 1 on Sunday 10/25, 12:00-13:30.

Just before their debut, on Saturday 10/3, KO1KEYZ also appeared on Fuji TV's "MUSIC FAIR" (18:00-18:30).
